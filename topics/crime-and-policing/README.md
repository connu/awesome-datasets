# Crime & Policing

Part of [Awesome Datasets](../../README.md). Entries here are generated from the `dataset-*.json` files in this directory - edit those, then run `python3 scripts/build.py`.

## United Kingdom

- [data.police.uk](https://data.police.uk/data/) - Street-level crime and outcome data for every police force in England, Wales and Northern Ireland, published monthly with anonymised coordinates and a free API.
  <br><sub>Home Office · CSV, REST API, JSON · ~10 GB full archive · 60 million+ crimes since 2010 · [Open Government Licence v3.0](https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [ONS Crime in England and Wales](https://www.ons.gov.uk/peoplepopulationandcommunity/crimeandjustice) - National crime statistics combining police recorded crime with the Crime Survey for England and Wales, covering trends, victim characteristics and fraud.
  <br><sub>Office for National Statistics · CSV, XLSX · Varies by release · Quarterly and annual series · [Open Government Licence v3.0](https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [Ministry of Justice Statistics](https://www.gov.uk/government/organisations/ministry-of-justice/about/statistics) - Criminal justice system statistics covering court proceedings, sentencing, prison population, probation, reoffending and legal aid.
  <br><sub>Ministry of Justice · CSV, XLSX, ODS · Multi-GB · Quarterly and annual series · [Open Government Licence v3.0](https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [Home Office Police Workforce Statistics](https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/police-workforce-england-and-wales) - Officer and staff headcounts by force, rank, function, ethnicity and gender, alongside joiners, leavers and diversity breakdowns.
  <br><sub>Home Office · CSV, ODS, XLSX · ~200 MB · Biannual series by force · [Open Government Licence v3.0](https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [UK Data Service Crime Surveys](https://ukdataservice.ac.uk/) - Archive of deposited social survey microdata including the Crime Survey for England and Wales, Offending Crime and Justice Survey and police force data.
  <br><sub>UK Data Service · Stata, SPSS, CSV, Tab-delimited · Varies by study · 9,000+ archived data collections · [End User Licence; registration and agreement required](https://ukdataservice.ac.uk/app/uploads/cd137-enduserlicence.pdf) · Signed agreement required · Non-commercial only</sub>

## United States

- [FBI Crime Data Explorer](https://cde.ucr.cjis.gov/) - National incident-based and summary crime statistics from participating law enforcement agencies, covering offences, arrests, clearances, hate crime and use of force.
  <br><sub>Federal Bureau of Investigation · CSV, REST API, JSON · ~20 GB · Millions of incidents per year from 18,000+ agencies · [US Government public domain](https://cde.ucr.cjis.gov/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [Bureau of Justice Statistics Data Collections](https://bjs.ojp.gov/data-collections) - National surveys and censuses of victimisation, prisons, jails, courts, prosecution and law enforcement agencies, with downloadable microdata.
  <br><sub>Bureau of Justice Statistics · CSV, SAS, Stata, ASCII · Multi-GB · 50+ ongoing data collections · [US Government public domain](https://bjs.ojp.gov/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [NYC Police Complaint Data](https://data.cityofnewyork.us/Public-Safety/NYPD-Complaint-Data-Historic/qgea-i56i) - Every felony, misdemeanour and violation complaint reported to the NYPD since 2006, with offence classification, coordinates, premises type and suspect and victim demographics.
  <br><sub>New York City Police Department · CSV, JSON, GeoJSON, REST API · ~2 GB · 9 million+ complaints since 2006 · [NYC Open Data Terms of Use](https://opendata.cityofnewyork.us/overview/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [Chicago Crimes Dataset](https://data.cityofchicago.org/Public-Safety/Crimes-2001-to-Present/ijzp-q8t2) - Reported crimes in Chicago from 2001 onward with block-level location, IUCR offence code, arrest flag, beat, district and community area.
  <br><sub>City of Chicago · CSV, JSON, GeoJSON, REST API · ~2 GB · 8 million+ reported crimes · [City of Chicago open data terms](https://www.chicago.gov/city/en/narr/foia/data_disclaimer.html) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [National Archive of Criminal Justice Data](https://www.icpsr.umich.edu/web/pages/NACJD/index.html) - Archive of thousands of criminal justice research datasets with documentation, spanning policing experiments, sentencing, corrections and victimisation studies.
  <br><sub>ICPSR, University of Michigan · Stata, SPSS, SAS, CSV · Varies by study · 3,000+ archived studies · [Varies by study; many require free registration](https://www.icpsr.umich.edu/web/pages/datamanagement/confidentiality/conf-terms.html) · Free account required · Commercial use: check terms</sub>
