# Taxi & Ride-Hailing

> Trip records, vehicle availability, fares and licensing for taxis and for-hire vehicles.

Part of [Awesome Datasets](../../README.md). Entries here are generated from the `dataset-*.json` files in this directory - edit those, then run `python3 scripts/build.py`.

## United Kingdom

- [TfL Unified API](https://api-portal.tfl.gov.uk/) - Transport for London's single API covering journey planning, live arrivals, disruptions, cycle hire docking stations, road status and air quality.
  <br><sub>Transport for London · REST API, JSON, XML · API · All London transport modes in real time · [TfL Open Data Licence, based on OGL](https://tfl.gov.uk/corporate/terms-and-conditions/transport-data-service) · Free account required · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [TfL Open Data](https://tfl.gov.uk/info-for/open-data-users/) - Bulk London transport datasets including cycle hire journeys, bus route sequences, station entry and exit counts, and taxi and private hire licensing.
  <br><sub>Transport for London · CSV, XML, GTFS, Shapefile · Multi-GB · Millions of cycle hire journeys and station counts · [TfL Open Data Licence, based on OGL](https://tfl.gov.uk/corporate/terms-and-conditions/transport-data-service) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [DfT Taxi and Private Hire Vehicle Statistics](https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/taxi-statistics) - Licensed taxi and private hire vehicle and driver counts by licensing authority, with wheelchair accessibility and licensing policy detail.
  <br><sub>Department for Transport · ODS, CSV · ~100 MB · Biennial series covering all English licensing authorities · [Open Government Licence v3.0](https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [London Datastore](https://data.london.gov.uk/) - The Greater London Authority's open data portal covering transport, housing, planning, demographics, economy and environment across the capital.
  <br><sub>Greater London Authority · CSV, GeoJSON, XLSX, REST API · Multi-GB · 700+ datasets · [Open Government Licence v3.0 for most datasets](https://data.london.gov.uk/) · Direct download · Commercial use: check terms</sub>
- [NaPTAN National Public Transport Access Nodes](https://beta-naptan.dft.gov.uk/) - The national register of every public transport access point in Great Britain, including bus stops, rail stations, taxi ranks and ferry terminals with coordinates.
  <br><sub>Department for Transport · CSV, XML, GeoJSON · ~500 MB · 435,000+ access nodes · [Open Government Licence v3.0](https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>

## Singapore

- [LTA DataMall](https://datamall.lta.gov.sg/content/datamall/en.html) - Singapore's transport data platform with real-time taxi availability, bus arrivals, traffic incidents, carpark availability, ERP rates and passenger volumes.
  <br><sub>Land Transport Authority · REST API, JSON, CSV, Shapefile · Varies by API · 60+ real-time and static datasets · [Singapore Open Data Licence](https://datamall.lta.gov.sg/content/datamall/en/SingaporeOpenDataLicence.html) · Free account required · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [data.gov.sg](https://data.gov.sg/) - Singapore's central open data portal covering transport, housing, economy, environment and population, with a consistent API across datasets.
  <br><sub>Government Technology Agency of Singapore · CSV, JSON, GeoJSON, REST API · Varies by dataset · 3,000+ datasets · [Singapore Open Data Licence 1.0](https://data.gov.sg/open-data-licence) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [LTA Statistics in Brief](https://www.lta.gov.sg/content/ltagov/en/who_we_are/statistics_and_publications/statistics.html) - Annual transport statistics covering taxi and private hire fleet size, vehicle population, public transport ridership and road network length.
  <br><sub>Land Transport Authority · PDF, XLSX, CSV · ~100 MB · Annual series with historical tables · [Singapore Open Data Licence](https://data.gov.sg/open-data-licence) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [OneMap API](https://www.onemap.gov.sg/apidocs/) - Singapore's authoritative mapping API providing geocoding, routing, planning-area boundaries, population queries and themed map layers.
  <br><sub>Singapore Land Authority · REST API, JSON, GeoJSON · API · National address and boundary coverage · [Singapore Open Data Licence with SLA attribution](https://www.onemap.gov.sg/legal/opendatalicence.html) · Free account required · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [Singapore Department of Statistics Tablebuilder](https://tablebuilder.singstat.gov.sg/) - Official statistics tables covering transport expenditure, household vehicle ownership, population and economic indicators, with an open API.
  <br><sub>Department of Statistics Singapore · REST API, CSV, XLSX · Varies by table · Thousands of statistical tables · [Singapore Open Data Licence](https://www.singstat.gov.sg/terms-of-use) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>

## United States

- [NYC TLC Trip Record Data](https://www.nyc.gov/site/tlc/about/tlc-trip-record-data.page) - Every yellow taxi, green taxi and high-volume for-hire trip in New York City since 2009, with timestamps, pickup and dropoff zones, distance and fare breakdown.
  <br><sub>NYC Taxi and Limousine Commission · Parquet · ~50 GB · Billions of trips since 2009 · [NYC Open Data Terms of Use](https://www.nyc.gov/home/terms-of-use.page) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [Chicago Taxi Trips](https://data.cityofchicago.org/Transportation/Taxi-Trips-2013-2023-/wrvz-psew) - Reported Chicago taxi trips with anonymised taxi identifier, timestamps, census tract, fare, tips, tolls and payment type.
  <br><sub>City of Chicago · CSV, JSON, REST API · ~40 GB · 200 million+ trips · [City of Chicago open data terms](https://www.chicago.gov/city/en/narr/foia/data_disclaimer.html) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [Chicago Transportation Network Providers Trips](https://data.cityofchicago.org/Transportation/Transportation-Network-Providers-Trips-2023-/n26f-ihde) - Ride-hailing trips reported by Uber, Lyft and Via in Chicago with pickup and dropoff tracts, fare, tip, shared-trip flag and trip duration.
  <br><sub>City of Chicago · CSV, JSON, REST API · ~50 GB · 300 million+ ride-hailing trips · [City of Chicago open data terms](https://www.chicago.gov/city/en/narr/foia/data_disclaimer.html) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [NYC TLC Licensed Vehicles and Drivers](https://data.cityofnewyork.us/Transportation/TLC-Vehicle-Licenses/pkmi-4kfn) - The register of licensed for-hire vehicles and medallions with base affiliation, vehicle type, wheelchair accessibility and licence expiry.
  <br><sub>NYC Taxi and Limousine Commission · CSV, JSON, REST API · ~500 MB · 100,000+ licensed vehicles · [NYC Open Data Terms of Use](https://opendata.cityofnewyork.us/overview/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [SFMTA and DataSF Mobility Data](https://datasf.org/opendata/) - San Francisco open data covering taxi trips, parking, curb regulations, scooter permits and transit performance.
  <br><sub>City and County of San Francisco · CSV, GeoJSON, REST API · Varies by dataset · 600+ published datasets · [San Francisco open data terms, public domain](https://datasf.org/opendata/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
