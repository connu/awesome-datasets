# Energy & Electricity

> Generation mix, grid demand, wholesale prices, network capacity and household consumption.

Part of [Awesome Datasets](../../README.md). Entries here are generated from the `dataset-*.json` files in this directory - edit those, then run `python3 scripts/build.py`.

## Germany

- [SMARD Strommarktdaten](https://www.smard.de/en) - The German regulator's electricity market platform publishing quarter-hourly generation by source, consumption, cross-border flows and wholesale prices.
  <br><sub>Bundesnetzagentur · CSV, XLSX, REST API · Multi-GB · Quarter-hourly series from 2015 · [CC BY 4.0](https://www.smard.de/en/datennutzung) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [Marktstammdatenregister](https://www.marktstammdatenregister.de/MaStR) - The statutory register of every electricity and gas generating unit in Germany, down to individual rooftop solar installations, with capacity, location and commissioning date.
  <br><sub>Bundesnetzagentur · XML, CSV, REST API · ~15 GB · 5 million+ registered units · [Datenlizenz Deutschland Namensnennung 2.0](https://www.govdata.de/dl-de/by-2-0) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [Open Power System Data](https://open-power-system-data.org/) - Cleaned and harmonised European power system data packages covering time series, conventional power plants, renewable installations and weather, ready for modelling.
  <br><sub>Open Power System Data consortium · CSV, SQLite, XLSX · ~5 GB · Multiple curated data packages · [MIT and CC BY 4.0 depending on package](https://open-power-system-data.org/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [ENTSO-E Transparency Platform](https://transparency.entsoe.eu/) - Pan-European electricity data mandated by EU regulation, covering load, generation, transmission capacity, outages and day-ahead prices for every bidding zone.
  <br><sub>European Network of Transmission System Operators for Electricity · REST API, CSV, XML · Multi-TB archive · All European bidding zones since 2015 · [ENTSO-E Transparency Platform terms, free reuse with attribution](https://transparency.entsoe.eu/) · Free account required · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [Energy-Charts](https://www.energy-charts.info/) - Interactive and downloadable German and European electricity production, price and emissions data maintained by a public research institute.
  <br><sub>Fraunhofer Institute for Solar Energy Systems ISE · CSV, JSON, REST API · Varies by series · Hourly series from 2002 · [CC BY 4.0](https://www.energy-charts.info/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>

## United Kingdom

- [Elexon BMRS Insights](https://bmrs.elexon.co.uk/) - Half-hourly balancing mechanism data for the GB electricity market, covering generation by fuel type, demand, imbalance prices and unit-level bids and offers.
  <br><sub>Elexon · REST API, CSV, XML · Multi-GB archive · Half-hourly settlement periods since 2001 · [Elexon open data terms, free reuse with attribution](https://bmrs.elexon.co.uk/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [National Grid ESO Data Portal](https://www.neso.energy/data-portal) - Great Britain's system operator data covering carbon intensity, embedded generation forecasts, demand forecasts, constraint costs and historic outturn.
  <br><sub>National Energy System Operator · CSV, JSON, REST API · Varies by dataset · 200+ published datasets · [NESO Open Data Licence, based on OGL](https://www.neso.energy/data-portal) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [Carbon Intensity API](https://carbonintensity.org.uk/) - Half-hourly actual and forecast carbon intensity of GB electricity, nationally and by region, with generation mix breakdown and a free unauthenticated API.
  <br><sub>National Grid ESO and University of Oxford · REST API, JSON · API · Half-hourly series from 2017 · [CC BY 4.0](https://github.com/carbon-intensity/terms) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [DESNZ Energy Statistics](https://www.gov.uk/government/organisations/department-for-energy-security-and-net-zero/about/statistics) - Official UK energy statistics including the Digest of UK Energy Statistics, sub-national gas and electricity consumption, and renewable capacity by technology.
  <br><sub>Department for Energy Security and Net Zero · XLSX, CSV, ODS · ~2 GB · Annual and quarterly series from 1970 · [Open Government Licence v3.0](https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [UK-DALE Household Electricity Demand](https://jack-kelly.com/data/) - Appliance-level and whole-house electricity demand recorded at up to 16 kHz in five UK homes, the reference dataset for non-intrusive load monitoring.
  <br><sub>Jack Kelly and William Knottenbelt, Imperial College London · HDF5, CSV, FLAC · ~800 GB (full), ~5 GB (6-second) · 5 households over up to 4.3 years · [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
