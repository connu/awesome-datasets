# Vehicles & Car Sales

> Vehicle registrations, sales volumes, specifications, fuel economy and recall records.

Part of [Awesome Datasets](../../README.md). Entries here are generated from the `dataset-*.json` files in this directory - edit those, then run `python3 scripts/build.py`.

## United Kingdom

- [DfT Vehicle Licensing Statistics](https://www.gov.uk/government/statistical-data-sets/all-vehicles-veh01) - Quarterly counts of licensed and registered vehicles in Great Britain broken down by body type, fuel, make, model, keepership and local authority.
  <br><sub>Department for Transport and DVLA · ODS, CSV · ~2 GB · Quarterly series from 1994 · [Open Government Licence v3.0](https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [DVLA Developer Portal](https://developer-portal.driver-vehicle-licensing.api.gov.uk/) - Official APIs for vehicle enquiry and MOT history, returning make, model, fuel type, tax and MOT status from a registration number.
  <br><sub>Driver and Vehicle Licensing Agency · REST API, JSON · API · 40 million+ licensed vehicles · [Open Government Licence v3.0 with API terms](https://developer-portal.driver-vehicle-licensing.api.gov.uk/) · Free account required · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [VCA Car Fuel and Emissions Data](https://carfueldata.vehicle-certification-agency.gov.uk/) - Official type-approval fuel consumption and CO2, NOx and particulate emissions figures for every new car model sold in the UK.
  <br><sub>Vehicle Certification Agency · CSV, XLSX · ~100 MB · Model-level data from 2000 · [Open Government Licence v3.0](https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [Electric Vehicle Charging Device Statistics](https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/electric-vehicle-charging-infrastructure-statistics) - Quarterly counts of public electric vehicle charging devices by local authority, speed rating and devices per head of population.
  <br><sub>Department for Transport · ODS, CSV · ~50 MB · Quarterly series by local authority since 2019 · [Open Government Licence v3.0](https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [SMMT Vehicle Data](https://www.smmt.co.uk/vehicle-data/) - Industry-body registration and production figures for UK cars, vans, trucks and buses, broken down by manufacturer, model and fuel type.
  <br><sub>Society of Motor Manufacturers and Traders · XLSX, PDF, Web · Small tabular files · Monthly and annual series by segment · [Free for reference; redistribution requires permission](https://www.smmt.co.uk/) · Direct download · Non-commercial only</sub>

## India

- [Vahan Dashboard](https://vahan.parivahan.gov.in/vahan4dashboard/) - Live national vehicle registration counts sliceable by state, RTO, maker, fuel type and vehicle class, sourced from the registration system itself.
  <br><sub>Ministry of Road Transport and Highways · Web dashboard, XLSX · Varies by query · 350 million+ registered vehicles · [Government Open Data License - India](https://www.data.gov.in/government-open-data-license-india) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [data.gov.in Transport Sector](https://www.data.gov.in/sector/transport) - Open government catalogue of transport datasets covering registered vehicles by category, permits, driving licences and state transport undertakings.
  <br><sub>National Informatics Centre · CSV, XLSX, JSON, REST API · Varies by resource · 3,000+ transport resources · [Government Open Data License - India](https://www.data.gov.in/government-open-data-license-india) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [MoRTH Road Transport Year Book](https://morth.nic.in/road-transport-year-books) - Annual national compendium of registered motor vehicles by state and category, along with fleet composition and transport revenue.
  <br><sub>Ministry of Road Transport and Highways · PDF, XLSX · ~50 MB per edition · Annual editions with state-level tables · [Government Open Data License - India](https://www.data.gov.in/government-open-data-license-india) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [SIAM Industry Statistics](https://www.siam.in/statistics.aspx) - Industry-body figures for domestic sales, production and exports of passenger vehicles, two-wheelers, three-wheelers and commercial vehicles.
  <br><sub>Society of Indian Automobile Manufacturers · XLSX, PDF · Small tabular files · Monthly and annual series by segment · [Free for reference; redistribution requires permission](https://www.siam.in/) · Direct download · Non-commercial only</sub>
- [Parivahan Analytics and Reports](https://parivahan.gov.in/parivahan/) - National transport portal exposing registration, permit, fitness and driving licence statistics from the Vahan and Sarathi systems.
  <br><sub>Ministry of Road Transport and Highways · Web dashboard, PDF, XLSX · Varies by report · National coverage across 1,300+ RTOs · [Government Open Data License - India](https://www.data.gov.in/government-open-data-license-india) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>

## United States

- [NHTSA vPIC API](https://vpic.nhtsa.dot.gov/api/) - Decodes any North American VIN into make, model, year, body class, engine and plant, and lists every manufacturer and equipment type on record.
  <br><sub>National Highway Traffic Safety Administration · REST API, JSON, XML, CSV · API; full DB export ~1 GB · 27,000+ makes and models · [US Government public domain](https://www.nhtsa.gov/nhtsa-datasets-and-apis) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [NHTSA Datasets and APIs](https://www.nhtsa.gov/nhtsa-datasets-and-apis) - Central index of federal vehicle safety data covering recalls, complaints, crash test ratings, investigations and child seat inspections.
  <br><sub>National Highway Traffic Safety Administration · REST API, CSV, JSON · Varies by feed · Millions of complaints and recall records · [US Government public domain](https://www.nhtsa.gov/nhtsa-datasets-and-apis) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [EPA Fuel Economy Data](https://www.fueleconomy.gov/feg/download.shtml) - Official city, highway and combined MPG ratings plus emissions and cost estimates for every vehicle sold in the US since 1984.
  <br><sub>US EPA and Department of Energy · CSV, XML, REST API · ~20 MB · 48,000+ vehicle configurations since 1984 · [US Government public domain](https://www.fueleconomy.gov/feg/download.shtml) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [FHWA Highway Statistics Series](https://www.fhwa.dot.gov/policyinformation/statistics.cfm) - Annual state-level tables of registered vehicles, licensed drivers, fuel consumption, road mileage and highway finance going back decades.
  <br><sub>Federal Highway Administration · XLSX, CSV, PDF · ~500 MB for the full archive · Annual series by state from 1945 · [US Government public domain](https://www.fhwa.dot.gov/policyinformation/statistics.cfm) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [BTS National Transportation Statistics](https://www.bts.gov/topics/national-transportation-statistics) - Cross-modal reference tables including new and used vehicle sales, fleet size, average vehicle age, and cost of owning and operating a car.
  <br><sub>Bureau of Transportation Statistics · XLSX, CSV · ~200 MB · 260+ statistical tables · [US Government public domain](https://www.bts.gov/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
