# Company Registries

> Legal entity records, directors, ownership, filings and business demography.

Part of [Awesome Datasets](../../README.md). Entries here are generated from the `dataset-*.json` files in this directory - edit those, then run `python3 scripts/build.py`.

## Contents

- [United Kingdom](#united-kingdom)

## United Kingdom

- [Companies House Public Data Product](https://download.companieshouse.gov.uk/en_output.html) - A monthly snapshot of every live company on the UK register with company number, registered address, SIC codes, incorporation date, status and accounts dates.
  <br><sub>Companies House · CSV, ZIP · ~2 GB · 5 million+ live companies · [Open Government Licence v3.0](https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [Companies House Public Data API](https://developer.company-information.service.gov.uk/) - REST access to company profiles, officers, persons with significant control, filing history, charges and insolvency records.
  <br><sub>Companies House · REST API, JSON · API · 5 million+ companies, 12 million+ officers · [Open Government Licence v3.0](https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/) · Free account required · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [Companies House Persons with Significant Control](https://download.companieshouse.gov.uk/en_pscdata.html) - The beneficial ownership register naming individuals and entities controlling UK companies, with nature of control and ownership percentage bands.
  <br><sub>Companies House · JSONL, ZIP · ~3 GB · 10 million+ PSC statements · [Open Government Licence v3.0](https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [ONS UK Business Activity and Demography](https://www.ons.gov.uk/businessindustryandtrade/business) - Statistics on business births, deaths, survival rates, enterprise counts by size and industry, and regional business population.
  <br><sub>Office for National Statistics · CSV, XLSX · Varies by release · Annual series by industry and region · [Open Government Licence v3.0](https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [OpenCorporates](https://opencorporates.com/) - The largest open database of companies worldwide, normalising registry records from 140+ jurisdictions into consistent entity and officer schemas.
  <br><sub>OpenCorporates · REST API, CSV, JSON · Multi-TB · 200 million+ companies · [Free API tier for open data reuse; bulk data requires a licence](https://opencorporates.com/) · Free account required · Commercial use: check terms</sub>

## India

- [MCA Company Master Data](https://www.mca.gov.in/content/mca/global/en/data-and-reports/company-llp-data/company-llp-master-data.html) - Registry extracts of every registered Indian company and LLP with CIN, incorporation date, registered state, class, category, authorised capital and status.
  <br><sub>Ministry of Corporate Affairs · CSV, XLSX · ~2 GB · 2 million+ registered entities · [Government Open Data License - India](https://www.data.gov.in/government-open-data-license-india) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [GST Portal Taxpayer Search](https://www.gst.gov.in/) - Registry lookup of GST-registered businesses with legal name, trade name, registration date, constitution, jurisdiction and filing status.
  <br><sub>Goods and Services Tax Network · Web, REST API · Varies by query · 14 million+ registered taxpayers · [Government terms of use; API access via GSP licensing](https://www.gst.gov.in/) · Free account required · Commercial use: check terms</sub>
- [data.gov.in Industries Sector](https://www.data.gov.in/sector/industries) - Open government catalogue of industrial and corporate datasets covering company registrations, MSME statistics, industrial production and factory data.
  <br><sub>National Informatics Centre · CSV, XLSX, JSON, REST API · Varies by resource · 5,000+ industry resources · [Government Open Data License - India](https://www.data.gov.in/government-open-data-license-india) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [Udyam Registration MSME Data](https://udyamregistration.gov.in/) - The national register of micro, small and medium enterprises with sector, investment band, employment and state distribution statistics.
  <br><sub>Ministry of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises · Web dashboard, XLSX, PDF · Varies by report · 50 million+ registered enterprises · [Government Open Data License - India](https://www.data.gov.in/government-open-data-license-india) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [Annual Survey of Industries](https://www.mospi.gov.in/web/mospi/annual-survey-of-industries) - The principal source of Indian factory-sector statistics, reporting output, inputs, capital, employment and wages by industry and state.
  <br><sub>Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation · PDF, XLSX, CSV · ~2 GB · 200,000+ factories surveyed annually · [Government Open Data License - India](https://www.data.gov.in/government-open-data-license-india) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
