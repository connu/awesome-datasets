# Agriculture & Crops

> Crop production, yields, prices, land use, irrigation and farm economics.

Part of [Awesome Datasets](../../README.md). Entries here are generated from the `dataset-*.json` files in this directory - edit those, then run `python3 scripts/build.py`.

## India

- [Agmarknet Commodity Prices](https://agmarknet.gov.in/) - Daily arrival volumes and minimum, maximum and modal prices for hundreds of commodities across thousands of regulated agricultural markets.
  <br><sub>Directorate of Marketing and Inspection, Ministry of Agriculture · CSV, Web, REST API · Multi-GB archive · 3,000+ markets, daily since 2001 · [Government Open Data License - India](https://www.data.gov.in/government-open-data-license-india) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [data.gov.in Agriculture Sector](https://www.data.gov.in/sector/agriculture) - Open government catalogue of agriculture datasets covering crop-wise area, production and yield by district, fertiliser use, irrigation and horticulture.
  <br><sub>National Informatics Centre · CSV, XLSX, JSON, REST API · Varies by resource · 10,000+ agriculture resources · [Government Open Data License - India](https://www.data.gov.in/government-open-data-license-india) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [Ministry of Agriculture Agricultural Statistics at a Glance](https://agriwelfare.gov.in/en/Agricultural_Statistics_at_a_Glance) - The annual national compendium of crop area, production, yield, prices, credit, machinery and land holdings by state and crop.
  <br><sub>Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare · PDF, XLSX · ~100 MB per edition · Annual editions with long historical series · [Government Open Data License - India](https://www.data.gov.in/government-open-data-license-india) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [UPAg Unified Portal for Agricultural Statistics](https://upag.gov.in/) - India's unified agricultural statistics portal consolidating crop area, production, yield, price, procurement and stock data from multiple ministries into one analytical interface.
  <br><sub>Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare · CSV, XLSX, Web dashboard · Varies by report · Crop-wise series across states and seasons · [Government Open Data License - India](https://www.data.gov.in/government-open-data-license-india) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [FAOSTAT India](https://www.fao.org/faostat/en/) - Harmonised international agricultural statistics covering production, trade, food balance sheets, land use, emissions and prices for India and 245 other territories.
  <br><sub>Food and Agriculture Organization of the United Nations · CSV, REST API, ZIP · ~20 GB · 245 territories, 1961 onward · [CC BY 4.0](https://www.fao.org/contact-us/terms/en/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>

## United States

- [USDA NASS Quick Stats](https://quickstats.nass.usda.gov/) - The full agricultural census and survey database with crop and livestock production, acreage, yield, price and farm economics down to county level.
  <br><sub>USDA National Agricultural Statistics Service · CSV, REST API · ~40 GB · 70 million+ observations since 1866 · [US Government public domain](https://www.nass.usda.gov/) · Free account required · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [USDA Cropland Data Layer](https://www.nass.usda.gov/Research_and_Science/Cropland/SARS1a.php) - Annual 30-metre raster classification of crop type across the continental United States, derived from satellite imagery and ground truth.
  <br><sub>USDA National Agricultural Statistics Service · GeoTIFF, Web service · ~10 GB per year · Continental US coverage from 1997 · [US Government public domain](https://www.nass.usda.gov/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [USDA Economic Research Service Data Products](https://www.ers.usda.gov/data-products) - Curated economic datasets covering farm income, food prices, food security, rural demographics, commodity outlook and international agricultural trade.
  <br><sub>USDA Economic Research Service · CSV, XLSX, REST API · Multi-GB · 200+ data products · [US Government public domain](https://www.ers.usda.gov/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [Census of Agriculture](https://www.nass.usda.gov/AgCensus/) - The quinquennial census of every US farm operation, reporting land use, production, sales, expenses, machinery, operator demographics and practices.
  <br><sub>USDA National Agricultural Statistics Service · CSV, PDF, REST API · ~5 GB · 1.9 million farms enumerated · [US Government public domain](https://www.nass.usda.gov/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [USDA Risk Management Agency Crop Insurance Data](https://www.rma.usda.gov/tools-reports/summary-business) - Summary of business files reporting insured acres, liability, premium, subsidy and indemnity payments by county, crop, practice and cause of loss.
  <br><sub>USDA Risk Management Agency · TXT, CSV, ZIP · ~10 GB · County-crop records since 1989 · [US Government public domain](https://www.rma.usda.gov/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
