# Real Estate & Housing

> Property transactions, price indices, rents, construction activity and housing stock characteristics.

Part of [Awesome Datasets](../../README.md). Entries here are generated from the `dataset-*.json` files in this directory - edit those, then run `python3 scripts/build.py`.

## Australia

- [ABS Residential Property Price Indexes](https://www.abs.gov.au/statistics/economy/price-indexes-and-inflation) - Official quarterly house and attached dwelling price indexes for the eight Australian capital cities, with total value of the dwelling stock.
  <br><sub>Australian Bureau of Statistics · XLSX, CSV, SDMX · ~200 MB · Quarterly series from 2003 · [CC BY 4.0](https://www.abs.gov.au/website-privacy-copyright-and-disclaimer) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [ABS Building Approvals](https://www.abs.gov.au/statistics/industry/building-and-construction) - Monthly counts and values of approved residential and non-residential building work by local government area, the leading indicator for Australian construction.
  <br><sub>Australian Bureau of Statistics · XLSX, CSV, SDMX · ~500 MB · Monthly series from 1983 · [CC BY 4.0](https://www.abs.gov.au/website-privacy-copyright-and-disclaimer) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [NSW Property Sales Information](https://valuation.property.nsw.gov.au/embed/propertySalesInformation) - Weekly and annual dumps of every property sale registered in New South Wales, with address, price, contract date, area and zoning.
  <br><sub>NSW Valuer General · DAT, ZIP, CSV · ~5 GB · Millions of sales since 1990 · [CC BY 4.0](https://www.nsw.gov.au/copyright) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [AIHW Housing Assistance Data](https://www.aihw.gov.au/reports-data/health-welfare-services/housing-assistance) - National social housing, homelessness services and rent assistance statistics with waiting lists, tenant demographics and service outcomes.
  <br><sub>Australian Institute of Health and Welfare · XLSX, CSV, PDF · ~1 GB · Annual national collections · [CC BY 4.0](https://www.aihw.gov.au/copyright) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [ABS Census of Population and Housing](https://www.abs.gov.au/census) - Five-yearly census tables on dwelling structure, tenure, mortgage and rent payments, household composition and occupancy down to statistical area level 1.
  <br><sub>Australian Bureau of Statistics · CSV, XLSX, TableBuilder · Multi-GB · 26 million+ people, 11 million+ dwellings · [CC BY 4.0](https://www.abs.gov.au/website-privacy-copyright-and-disclaimer) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>

## United Kingdom

- [HM Land Registry Price Paid Data](https://www.gov.uk/government/statistical-data-sets/price-paid-data-downloads) - Every residential property sale registered in England and Wales since 1995, with full address, price, date, property type and tenure.
  <br><sub>HM Land Registry · CSV, Linked Data API · ~4 GB complete file · 28 million+ transactions since 1995 · [Open Government Licence v3.0](https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [UK House Price Index](https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/uk-house-price-index-reports) - Official monthly hedonic house price index down to local authority level, split by property type, buyer status and funding method.
  <br><sub>HM Land Registry and ONS · CSV, Linked Data · ~200 MB · Monthly series from 1968 · [Open Government Licence v3.0](https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [Energy Performance of Buildings Data](https://epc.opendatacommunities.org/) - Energy performance certificates for domestic and non-domestic buildings, giving floor area, construction age, heating type, insulation and efficiency ratings by address.
  <br><sub>Department for Levelling Up, Housing and Communities · CSV, REST API · ~10 GB · 25 million+ certificates · [Open Government Licence v3.0](https://epc.opendatacommunities.org/docs/copyright) · Free account required · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [ONS Housing Statistics](https://www.ons.gov.uk/peoplepopulationandcommunity/housing) - National statistics on housing affordability, private rental prices, dwelling stock, second homes and household composition.
  <br><sub>Office for National Statistics · CSV, XLSX, REST API · Varies by series · Hundreds of published series · [Open Government Licence v3.0](https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [Ordnance Survey Open Data](https://osdatahub.os.uk/downloads/open) - Free national mapping products including building outlines, postcode centroids, road networks and administrative boundaries, the geographic base layer for UK property analysis.
  <br><sub>Ordnance Survey · GeoPackage, Shapefile, CSV, Vector tiles · Up to ~20 GB per product · National coverage of Great Britain · [Open Government Licence v3.0 with OS attribution](https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>

## United States

- [FHFA House Price Index](https://www.fhfa.gov/data/hpi) - Repeat-sales house price indices for the nation, states, metros and ZIP codes, built from conforming mortgage transactions since 1975.
  <br><sub>Federal Housing Finance Agency · CSV, XLSX · ~100 MB · Quarterly and monthly series from 1975 · [US Government public domain](https://www.fhfa.gov/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [American Housing Survey](https://www.census.gov/programs-surveys/ahs.html) - Detailed microdata on housing units and their occupants covering size, age, condition, costs, mortgages, neighbourhood quality and recent moves.
  <br><sub>US Census Bureau and HUD · CSV, SAS, DAT · ~2 GB per biennial wave · ~60,000 housing units per wave · [US Government public domain](https://www.census.gov/about/policies/open-gov.html) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [HUD Open Data](https://hudgis-hud.opendata.arcgis.com/) - Geospatial and tabular housing data covering public housing developments, fair market rents, low-income housing tax credit properties and homelessness counts.
  <br><sub>US Department of Housing and Urban Development · GeoJSON, Shapefile, CSV, REST API · Varies by layer · 200+ published datasets · [US Government public domain](https://www.hud.gov/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [HMDA Loan Application Register](https://ffiec.cfpb.gov/data-browser/) - Every reported mortgage application in the country with loan amount, purpose, outcome, applicant demographics and census-tract location.
  <br><sub>Consumer Financial Protection Bureau · CSV, REST API · ~10 GB per year · ~15 million applications per year · [US Government public domain](https://ffiec.cfpb.gov/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [Census Building Permits Survey](https://www.census.gov/construction/bps/) - Monthly and annual counts of new residential construction permits by place, county and metro area, a standard leading indicator for housing supply.
  <br><sub>US Census Bureau · CSV, TXT · ~500 MB · Monthly series from 1959 · [US Government public domain](https://www.census.gov/about/policies/open-gov.html) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
