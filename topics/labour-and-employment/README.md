# Labour & Employment

> Employment levels, wages, job vacancies, occupational classifications and workforce demographics.

Part of [Awesome Datasets](../../README.md). Entries here are generated from the `dataset-*.json` files in this directory - edit those, then run `python3 scripts/build.py`.

## Canada

- [Statistics Canada Web Data Service](https://www.statcan.gc.ca/en/developers) - REST API and bulk downloads for every Statistics Canada table, covering labour force, income, demographics, trade and industry.
  <br><sub>Statistics Canada · REST API, CSV, JSON, SDMX · Varies by table · 8,000+ data tables · [Statistics Canada Open Licence](https://www.statcan.gc.ca/en/reference/licence) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [Labour Force Survey (Canada)](https://www.statcan.gc.ca/en/survey/household/3701) - Monthly survey producing Canada's official unemployment rate, with detail by province, industry, occupation, age, gender and full or part-time status.
  <br><sub>Statistics Canada · CSV, SDMX, PDF · Multi-GB · 56,000 households surveyed monthly · [Statistics Canada Open Licence](https://www.statcan.gc.ca/en/reference/licence) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [Open Government Canada](https://open.canada.ca/en/open-data) - Federal open data portal covering employment programmes, immigration, procurement, geospatial data and departmental performance.
  <br><sub>Government of Canada · CSV, JSON, XML, Shapefile, REST API · Varies by dataset · 40,000+ datasets · [Open Government Licence - Canada 2.0](https://open.canada.ca/en/open-government-licence-canada) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [Job Bank Labour Market Information](https://www.jobbank.gc.ca/trend-analysis) - Occupational outlooks, wage ranges by region, and in-demand skill profiles derived from job postings and administrative labour market data.
  <br><sub>Employment and Social Development Canada · CSV, XLSX, Web · Varies by product · 500+ occupations across regions · [Open Government Licence - Canada 2.0](https://open.canada.ca/en/open-government-licence-canada) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [National Occupational Classification](https://noc.esdc.gc.ca/) - Canada's official taxonomy of occupations with hierarchical codes, duties, employment requirements and example job titles used across immigration and labour policy.
  <br><sub>Employment and Social Development Canada and Statistics Canada · CSV, XLSX, Web · ~50 MB · 500+ unit groups, 30,000+ job titles · [Open Government Licence - Canada 2.0](https://open.canada.ca/en/open-government-licence-canada) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>

## United Kingdom

- [ONS Labour Market Statistics](https://www.ons.gov.uk/employmentandlabourmarket) - The UK's headline employment, unemployment, inactivity, vacancy and earnings statistics, published monthly with regional and industry detail.
  <br><sub>Office for National Statistics · CSV, XLSX, REST API · Multi-GB · Monthly series from 1971 · [Open Government Licence v3.0](https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [Annual Survey of Hours and Earnings](https://www.ons.gov.uk/employmentandlabourmarket/peopleinwork/earningsandworkinghours) - The authoritative UK earnings survey giving hourly and weekly pay distributions by occupation, industry, region, age and gender.
  <br><sub>Office for National Statistics · XLSX, CSV · ~2 GB · ~180,000 employee records annually · [Open Government Licence v3.0](https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [Gender Pay Gap Service Data](https://gender-pay-gap.service.gov.uk/) - Mandatory gender pay gap reports from every UK employer with 250 or more staff, with mean and median gaps, bonus gaps and quartile distributions.
  <br><sub>Government Equalities Office · CSV, REST API · ~200 MB · 10,000+ employers reporting annually since 2017 · [Open Government Licence v3.0](https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [Standard Occupational Classification](https://www.ons.gov.uk/methodology/classificationsandstandards/standardoccupationalclassificationsoc) - The UK's official occupation taxonomy with hierarchical codes, job title indexes and qualification levels, used across labour market and immigration policy.
  <br><sub>Office for National Statistics · XLSX, CSV · ~50 MB · 400+ unit groups, 30,000+ job titles · [Open Government Licence v3.0](https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [DWP Stat-Xplore](https://stat-xplore.dwp.gov.uk/) - Query and API access to benefit caseload statistics covering Universal Credit, disability benefits, pensions and jobseeker support by geography and claimant characteristic.
  <br><sub>Department for Work and Pensions · REST API, CSV, XLSX · Multi-GB · Monthly caseloads for millions of claimants · [Open Government Licence v3.0](https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/) · Free account required · Commercial use permitted</sub>

## United States

- [BLS Public Data API](https://www.bls.gov/developers/) - Programmatic access to every published BLS series including unemployment, payroll employment, wages, productivity and price indices.
  <br><sub>Bureau of Labor Statistics · REST API, JSON, TXT · API · 500,000+ time series · [US Government public domain](https://www.bls.gov/bls/linksite.htm) · Free account required · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [Current Population Survey](https://www.census.gov/programs-surveys/cps.html) - The monthly household survey behind the official unemployment rate, with microdata on labour force status, hours, earnings and demographics.
  <br><sub>US Census Bureau and BLS · CSV, Fixed-width, SAS · ~20 GB · 60,000 households surveyed monthly since 1940 · [US Government public domain](https://www.census.gov/about/policies/open-gov.html) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [O*NET Database](https://www.onetcenter.org/database.html) - Structured descriptions of nearly a thousand occupations covering required skills, knowledge, abilities, work activities, tools, technologies and job zones.
  <br><sub>US Department of Labor · CSV, SQL, XLSX, REST API · ~200 MB · 900+ occupations with 250+ descriptors each · [CC BY 4.0](https://www.onetcenter.org/license_db.html) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [Quarterly Census of Employment and Wages](https://www.bls.gov/cew/) - A near-census of employment and wages from unemployment insurance filings, available by county, industry and ownership at monthly frequency.
  <br><sub>Bureau of Labor Statistics · CSV, REST API · ~20 GB · 12 million+ establishments covered · [US Government public domain](https://www.bls.gov/bls/linksite.htm) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [Census LEHD LODES](https://lehd.ces.census.gov/data/) - Origin-destination employment statistics linking where workers live to where they work at census block level, with job counts by age, earnings and industry.
  <br><sub>US Census Bureau · CSV, GZIP · ~50 GB · Block-level job flows for all states since 2002 · [US Government public domain](https://lehd.ces.census.gov/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
