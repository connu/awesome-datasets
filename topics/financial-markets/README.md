# Financial Markets

> Securities prices, corporate filings, exchange reference data and market microstructure records.

Part of [Awesome Datasets](../../README.md). Entries here are generated from the `dataset-*.json` files in this directory - edit those, then run `python3 scripts/build.py`.

## India

- [NSE India Historical Data](https://www.nseindia.com/reports-indices-historical-index-data) - Historical index values, bhavcopy end-of-day quotes, derivatives contract data and securities reference information for India's largest exchange.
  <br><sub>National Stock Exchange of India · CSV, XLSX, ZIP · Multi-GB archive · Daily records since 1994 · [NSE terms of use; redistribution restricted](https://www.nseindia.com/) · Direct download · Non-commercial only</sub>
- [BSE India Historical Data](https://www.bseindia.com/markets/MarketInfo/BhavCopy.aspx) - Daily bhavcopy files with open, high, low, close, volume and turnover for every scrip listed on the Bombay Stock Exchange.
  <br><sub>BSE Limited · CSV, ZIP · Multi-GB archive · Daily records for 5,000+ listed securities · [BSE terms of use; redistribution restricted](https://www.bseindia.com/) · Direct download · Non-commercial only</sub>
- [RBI Database on Indian Economy](https://data.rbi.org.in/) - The central bank's statistical warehouse covering money supply, banking aggregates, interest rates, exchange rates, balance of payments and state finances.
  <br><sub>Reserve Bank of India · CSV, XLSX, Web query · Multi-GB · Thousands of macro-financial series · [Free reuse with attribution to RBI](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/Copyright.aspx) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [SEBI Reports and Statistics](https://www.sebi.gov.in/sebiweb/home/HomeAction.do?doListing=yes&sid=5&ssid=22&smid=0) - Regulator statistics on market turnover, mutual fund flows, foreign portfolio investment, IPO activity and registered intermediaries.
  <br><sub>Securities and Exchange Board of India · XLSX, PDF, CSV · ~1 GB · Monthly and annual series · [Free reuse with attribution to SEBI](https://www.sebi.gov.in/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [MCA Corporate Filings Data](https://www.mca.gov.in/content/mca/global/en/data-and-reports/company-statistics.html) - Statistics and open datasets on registered Indian companies including incorporations, closures, sector distribution and authorised capital.
  <br><sub>Ministry of Corporate Affairs · CSV, XLSX, PDF · ~2 GB · 2 million+ registered companies · [Government Open Data License - India](https://www.data.gov.in/government-open-data-license-india) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>

## United States

- [SEC EDGAR Full-Text Search and Filings](https://www.sec.gov/search-filings) - Every registration statement, annual report, quarterly report and insider transaction filed with the SEC, with structured XBRL financial statement data.
  <br><sub>US Securities and Exchange Commission · HTML, XBRL, JSON, REST API · ~5 TB full archive · 20 million+ filings since 1993 · [US Government public domain](https://www.sec.gov/privacy#dissemination) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [SEC Financial Statement Data Sets](https://www.sec.gov/data-research/financial-statement-data-sets) - Quarterly flat files extracting every numeric fact from XBRL exhibits in corporate filings, letting you build a fundamentals database without parsing XBRL yourself.
  <br><sub>US Securities and Exchange Commission · TSV, ZIP · ~20 GB · Quarterly sets from 2009 onward · [US Government public domain](https://www.sec.gov/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [Federal Reserve H.15 and Data Download Program](https://www.federalreserve.gov/datadownload/) - Official interest rates, exchange rates, money stock, industrial production and bank assets published directly by the Federal Reserve Board.
  <br><sub>Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System · CSV, XML, SDMX · Varies by series · Thousands of series, many daily since 1962 · [US Government public domain](https://www.federalreserve.gov/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [FINRA and SEC Market Structure Data](https://www.sec.gov/data-research/market-structure) - Aggregated equity market metrics on trade sizes, speeds, hidden liquidity, odd lots and off-exchange volume, derived from consolidated audit trail sources.
  <br><sub>US Securities and Exchange Commission · CSV, ZIP · ~50 GB · Daily security-level metrics since 2012 · [US Government public domain](https://www.sec.gov/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [US Treasury Fiscal Data API](https://fiscaldata.treasury.gov/api-documentation/) - Machine-readable federal financial data covering debt outstanding, auction results, interest rates, exchange rates and daily Treasury statements.
  <br><sub>US Department of the Treasury · REST API, JSON, CSV, XML · API · 80+ datasets with daily updates · [US Government public domain](https://fiscaldata.treasury.gov/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
