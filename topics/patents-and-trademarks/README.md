## European Union

- [EPO Open Patent Services](https://www.epo.org/en/searching-for-patents/data/web-services/ops) - The European Patent Office's REST API for bibliographic data, full text, legal status, families and citations across worldwide patent collections.
  <br><sub>European Patent Office · REST API, XML, JSON · API · 140 million+ patent documents · [EPO terms of use; free tier with registration](https://www.epo.org/en) · Free account required · Commercial use: check terms</sub>
- [EPO Worldwide Patent Statistical Database (PATSTAT)](https://www.epo.org/en/searching-for-patents/business/patstat) - The reference bibliographic database for patent statistics research, with harmonised applicants, citations, families and legal events across 100+ offices.
  <br><sub>European Patent Office · SQL dump, CSV · ~500 GB · 100 million+ patent applications · [EPO licence; free online edition for academic use, paid for full editions](https://www.epo.org/en/searching-for-patents/business/patstat) · Signed agreement required · Commercial use: check terms</sub>
- [EUIPO Open Data](https://www.euipo.europa.eu/en/open-data) - Bulk and API access to EU trade mark and registered Community design registers, including applicant, classification, status and opposition history.
  <br><sub>European Union Intellectual Property Office · XML, CSV, REST API · ~100 GB · 2 million+ EU trade marks and 1.7 million designs · [EUIPO open data terms, free reuse with attribution](https://www.euipo.europa.eu/en/open-data) · Free account required · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [EU Open Data Portal](https://data.europa.eu/en) - The official portal for European open data, federating datasets from EU institutions and all member state national portals under one search and API.
  <br><sub>Publications Office of the European Union · CSV, JSON, RDF, SPARQL, REST API · Varies by dataset · 1.7 million+ datasets · [Varies; commonly CC BY 4.0](https://data.europa.eu/en) · Direct download · Commercial use: check terms</sub>
- [Espacenet Bulk and Linked Data](https://worldwide.espacenet.com/) - The EPO's public patent search covering worldwide collections with full-text, family and legal-status views, backed by downloadable result sets.
  <br><sub>European Patent Office · XML, CSV, PDF · Varies by query · 150 million+ patent documents · [EPO terms of use](https://www.epo.org/en) · Direct download · Commercial use: check terms</sub>

## Japan

- [J-PlatPat](https://www.j-platpat.inpit.go.jp/) - The Japan Patent Office's public search platform for patents, utility models, designs and trademarks, with full text, legal status and family information.
  <br><sub>INPIT and Japan Patent Office · Web, PDF, XML · Varies by query · 100 million+ IP documents · [JPO terms of use, free reuse with attribution](https://www.j-platpat.inpit.go.jp/) · Direct download · Commercial use: check terms</sub>
- [JPO Statistics and Annual Reports](https://www.jpo.go.jp/e/resources/statistics/) - Official Japanese IP statistics covering applications, grants, examination pendency, appeals and international filings by technology field.
  <br><sub>Japan Patent Office · XLSX, PDF, CSV · ~500 MB · Annual series with long historical coverage · [Japan Government Standard Terms of Use, CC BY compatible](https://www.jpo.go.jp/e/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [INPIT IP Data Services](https://www.inpit.go.jp/english/) - Bulk and API distribution of Japanese patent, design and trademark data for research and commercial IP analytics platforms.
  <br><sub>National Center for Industrial Property Information and Training · XML, REST API, ZIP · Multi-TB · Full Japanese IP corpus · [INPIT terms; some products require a paid agreement](https://www.inpit.go.jp/english/) · Free account required · Commercial use: check terms</sub>
- [WIPO IP Statistics Data Center](https://www.wipo.int/ipstats/en/) - Harmonised international IP filing, grant and validity statistics for Japan and every other member state, comparable across offices and decades.
  <br><sub>World Intellectual Property Organization · CSV, XLSX, Web · ~5 GB · Annual series from 1980 for 200+ offices · [CC BY 3.0 IGO](https://www.wipo.int/tools/en/disclaim.html) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [Japan Open Data Portal IP Datasets](https://www.data.go.jp/) - The national open data catalogue including METI and JPO datasets on industrial property, standards, and innovation indicators.
  <br><sub>Digital Agency, Government of Japan · CSV, XLSX, JSON, REST API · Varies by dataset · 30,000+ datasets · [Japan Government Standard Terms of Use, CC BY compatible](https://www.data.go.jp/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>

## United States

- [USPTO PatentsView](https://patentsview.org/) - A normalised relational database of US patents with disambiguated inventors and assignees, citations, CPC classifications and geocoded locations.
  <br><sub>USPTO and PatentsView · CSV, TSV, REST API, PostgreSQL dump · ~100 GB · 8 million+ granted patents since 1976 · [CC BY 4.0](https://patentsview.org/) · Free account required · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [USPTO Open Data Portal](https://developer.uspto.gov/) - Official APIs and bulk products covering patent and trademark full text, examination file wrappers, assignments, and PTAB proceedings.
  <br><sub>US Patent and Trademark Office · REST API, XML, JSON, ZIP · Multi-TB · Full US patent and trademark corpus · [US Government public domain](https://developer.uspto.gov/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [USPTO Open Data Bulk Products](https://data.uspto.gov/) - Weekly full-text XML dumps of granted patents, published applications, trademark registrations and assignment records for local ingestion.
  <br><sub>US Patent and Trademark Office · XML, ZIP, DTD · ~5 TB full archive · Weekly issues since 1976 · [US Government public domain](https://data.uspto.gov/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [USPTO Trademark Case Files Dataset](https://www.uspto.gov/ip-policy/economic-research/research-datasets) - Research-grade extracts of trademark applications and registrations with owner, mark text, classification, prosecution events and outcomes.
  <br><sub>USPTO Office of the Chief Economist · CSV, Stata, ZIP · ~20 GB · 12 million+ trademark case files since 1870 · [US Government public domain](https://www.uspto.gov/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [Google Patents Public Data on BigQuery](https://console.cloud.google.com/marketplace/product/google_patents_public_datasets/google-patents-public-data) - Normalised worldwide patent bibliographic and full-text data joined across offices, queryable in BigQuery with citation and family linkages.
  <br><sub>Google and IFI CLAIMS · BigQuery · Multi-TB · 120 million+ patent publications worldwide · [CC BY 4.0 for the public dataset components](https://console.cloud.google.com/marketplace/product/google_patents_public_datasets/google-patents-public-data) · Free account required · Commercial use: check terms</sub>
