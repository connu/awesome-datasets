Part of [Awesome Datasets](../../README.md). Entries here are generated from the `dataset-*.json` files in this directory - edit those, then run `python3 scripts/build.py`.

## United Kingdom

- [NHS England Statistics](https://www.england.nhs.uk/statistics/) - Monthly operational statistics for the English NHS covering A&E attendances, referral to treatment waits, diagnostics, cancer waiting times and bed occupancy.
  <br><sub>NHS England · CSV, XLSX · Multi-GB · Monthly series by trust and commissioner · [Open Government Licence v3.0](https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [NHS Business Services Authority Prescription Data](https://opendata.nhsbsa.net/) - Every prescription item dispensed in English primary care, by practice, BNF chemical, quantity and cost, published monthly.
  <br><sub>NHS Business Services Authority · CSV, REST API · ~50 GB · 1 billion+ prescription items per year · [Open Government Licence v3.0](https://opendata.nhsbsa.net/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [NHS Digital Data Catalogue](https://digital.nhs.uk/data-and-information/data-collections-and-data-sets) - Catalogue of national health data collections including Hospital Episode Statistics, mental health services, maternity and community services datasets.
  <br><sub>NHS England Digital · CSV, XLSX, PDF · Varies by collection · Hundreds of national collections · [Open Government Licence v3.0 for published statistics](https://digital.nhs.uk/about-nhs-digital/terms-and-conditions) · Direct download · Commercial use: check terms</sub>
- [ONS Health and Life Expectancy](https://www.ons.gov.uk/peoplepopulationandcommunity/healthandsocialcare) - Official statistics on mortality, life expectancy, cause of death, disability, health inequality and avoidable deaths, down to local authority level.
  <br><sub>Office for National Statistics · CSV, XLSX, REST API · Varies by series · Hundreds of published series · [Open Government Licence v3.0](https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [OpenPrescribing](https://openprescribing.net/) - A searchable analytics layer over English primary care prescribing with standardised measures, practice comparisons and a public API.
  <br><sub>Bennett Institute for Applied Data Science, University of Oxford · REST API, CSV, JSON · Varies by query · Monthly data for 6,000+ GP practices · [MIT (code); prescribing data under OGL v3.0](https://openprescribing.net/about/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>

## United States

- [CMS Medicare Provider Data](https://data.cms.gov/) - Open Medicare data covering provider utilisation and payments, hospital quality measures, drug spending, nursing home ratings and enrolment.
  <br><sub>Centers for Medicare and Medicaid Services · CSV, JSON, REST API · Multi-GB · Hundreds of datasets, billions of claim-derived rows · [US Government public domain](https://data.cms.gov/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [openFDA](https://open.fda.gov/) - APIs over FDA adverse event reports, drug labels, recalls, device reports and NDC product listings, with full historical downloads.
  <br><sub>US Food and Drug Administration · REST API, JSON · ~100 GB full downloads · 20 million+ adverse event reports · [US Government public domain, CC0-equivalent](https://open.fda.gov/license/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [CDC BRFSS](https://www.cdc.gov/brfss/index.html) - The world's largest continuously conducted health survey, collecting risk behaviours, chronic conditions and preventive service use from US adults by state.
  <br><sub>Centers for Disease Control and Prevention · SAS, ASCII, CSV · ~10 GB · 400,000+ interviews per year since 1984 · [US Government public domain](https://www.cdc.gov/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [HealthData.gov](https://healthdata.gov/) - Federal catalogue of health datasets from HHS agencies, covering hospital capacity, provider directories, community health and public health surveillance.
  <br><sub>US Department of Health and Human Services · CSV, JSON, REST API · Varies by dataset · 3,000+ datasets · [US Government public domain](https://healthdata.gov/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [NHANES](https://www.cdc.gov/nchs/nhanes/index.htm) - Survey combining interviews with physical examinations and laboratory tests, giving objectively measured biomarkers, diet and body composition for a national sample.
  <br><sub>National Center for Health Statistics · SAS XPORT, CSV · ~20 GB · ~5,000 examined participants per year · [US Government public domain](https://www.cdc.gov/nchs/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
