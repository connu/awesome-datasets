# Public Transit

> Timetables, network topology, real-time vehicle positions and ridership counts for public transport.

Part of [Awesome Datasets](../../README.md). Entries here are generated from the `dataset-*.json` files in this directory - edit those, then run `python3 scripts/build.py`.

## Germany

- [Mobilithek](https://mobilithek.info/) - Germany's national access point for mobility data, brokering GTFS timetables, real-time feeds, roadworks, parking and traffic data from federal and state providers.
  <br><sub>Bundesministerium für Digitales und Verkehr · GTFS, GTFS-RT, DATEX II, NeTEx, REST API · Varies by feed · Hundreds of mobility data offers · [Varies by provider; commonly CC BY 4.0 or DL-DE-BY](https://mobilithek.info/) · Free account required · Commercial use: check terms</sub>
- [Deutsche Bahn Open Data](https://data.deutschebahn.com/) - Rail network and station data including station facilities, platform geometry, lifts and escalators, parking, and travel centre locations.
  <br><sub>Deutsche Bahn AG · CSV, JSON, GeoJSON, REST API · Varies by dataset · 100+ datasets covering 5,400 stations · [CC BY 4.0 and Deutsche Bahn Datenlizenz](https://data.deutschebahn.com/) · Direct download · Commercial use: check terms</sub>
- [GTFS.de Feeds](https://gtfs.de/) - Consolidated GTFS feeds covering long-distance, regional and local public transport across Germany, rebuilt regularly from official sources.
  <br><sub>gtfs.de · GTFS, GTFS-RT · ~500 MB per full feed · National coverage of German public transport · [CC BY 4.0](https://gtfs.de/en/feeds/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [GovData Germany](https://www.govdata.de/) - Federal open data portal aggregating datasets from all sixteen states, with transport, environment, population and administration categories.
  <br><sub>Bundesministerium des Innern · CSV, JSON, XML, WMS · Varies by dataset · 100,000+ datasets · [Varies; commonly DL-DE-BY-2.0 or CC BY 4.0](https://www.govdata.de/informationen/lizenzen) · Direct download · Commercial use: check terms</sub>
- [VBB Open Data](https://unternehmen.vbb.de/en/digital-services/datasets/) - Berlin and Brandenburg transport association data covering GTFS timetables, fare zones, stop registers and a departure-monitor API.
  <br><sub>Verkehrsverbund Berlin-Brandenburg · GTFS, CSV, REST API · ~200 MB · 13,000+ stops across Berlin-Brandenburg · [CC BY 4.0 (Datenlizenz Deutschland Namensnennung)](https://unternehmen.vbb.de/en/digital-services/datasets/) · Free account required · Commercial use permitted</sub>

## United States

- [National Transit Database](https://www.transit.dot.gov/ntd) - Mandatory annual and monthly reporting from every federally funded transit agency, covering ridership, revenue miles, fleet, safety events and operating cost.
  <br><sub>Federal Transit Administration · XLSX, CSV · ~1 GB · 3,000+ reporting agencies · [US Government public domain](https://www.transit.dot.gov/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [MTA Open Data](https://data.ny.gov/browse?Dataset-Information_Agency=Metropolitan+Transportation+Authority) - New York's subway, bus, commuter rail and bridge data including turnstile entries, hourly ridership, service alerts and on-time performance.
  <br><sub>Metropolitan Transportation Authority · CSV, JSON, REST API · Multi-GB · Hundreds of datasets · [NY State open data terms](https://data.ny.gov/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [TransitFeeds GTFS Archive](https://transitfeeds.com/) - Historical archive of GTFS schedule feeds from agencies worldwide, letting you reconstruct what a transit network looked like on any past date.
  <br><sub>TransitFeeds / MobilityData · GTFS, ZIP, REST API · Hundreds of GB · 1,000+ agencies with historical snapshots · [Feeds carry their agency licenses](https://transitfeeds.com/) · Direct download · Commercial use: check terms</sub>
- [BTS Transit Profiles](https://www.bts.gov/browse-statistical-products-and-data) - Cross-modal statistical products covering passenger travel, transit performance, freight movement and transportation economics.
  <br><sub>Bureau of Transportation Statistics · CSV, XLSX, REST API · Varies by product · Hundreds of statistical products · [US Government public domain](https://www.bts.gov/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [SFMTA Open Data](https://data.sfgov.org/browse?tags=sfmta) - San Francisco transit and streets data including Muni routes, stops, passenger counts, speed data, parking meters and taxi trips.
  <br><sub>San Francisco Municipal Transportation Agency · CSV, GeoJSON, REST API · Varies by dataset · 100+ transport datasets · [San Francisco open data terms, public domain](https://datasf.org/opendata/terms-of-use/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
