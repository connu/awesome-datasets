# Education & Schools

> Enrolment, attainment, institution directories, funding and workforce data for schools and universities.

Part of [Awesome Datasets](../../README.md). Entries here are generated from the `dataset-*.json` files in this directory - edit those, then run `python3 scripts/build.py`.

## Contents

- [India](#india)
- [United States](#united-states)

## India

- [UDISE+ School Education Statistics](https://udiseplus.gov.in/) - India's unified school information system with enrolment, teachers, infrastructure, drinking water, toilets and electricity for every recognised school.
  <br><sub>Ministry of Education · XLSX, PDF, Web dashboard · Multi-GB · 1.5 million+ schools, 250 million+ students · [Government Open Data License - India](https://www.data.gov.in/government-open-data-license-india) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [All India Survey on Higher Education](https://aishe.gov.in/aishe/home) - Annual census of Indian higher education institutions covering enrolment by level and discipline, faculty, infrastructure and finances.
  <br><sub>Ministry of Education · PDF, XLSX · ~1 GB · 1,100+ universities, 45,000+ colleges · [Government Open Data License - India](https://www.data.gov.in/government-open-data-license-india) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [data.gov.in Education Sector](https://www.data.gov.in/sector/education) - Open government catalogue of education datasets covering literacy rates, dropout rates, school infrastructure, scholarships and examination results.
  <br><sub>National Informatics Centre · CSV, XLSX, JSON, REST API · Varies by resource · 5,000+ education resources · [Government Open Data License - India](https://www.data.gov.in/government-open-data-license-india) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [NIRF India Rankings Data](https://www.nirfindia.org/Rankings/2024/Ranking.html) - Official ranking framework data for Indian institutions with underlying scores for teaching, research, graduation outcomes, outreach and perception.
  <br><sub>National Institutional Ranking Framework, Ministry of Education · PDF, XLSX · ~200 MB · Annual rankings across 13 categories · [Government Open Data License - India](https://www.data.gov.in/government-open-data-license-india) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [ASER Annual Status of Education Report](https://asercentre.org/) - Household-based survey measuring basic reading and arithmetic ability of rural Indian children, run annually across nearly every rural district.
  <br><sub>ASER Centre, Pratham · PDF, XLSX, Stata · ~500 MB · 600,000+ children surveyed per round · [Free for research and non-commercial use with attribution](https://asercentre.org/) · Direct download · Non-commercial only</sub>

## United States

- [IPEDS Integrated Postsecondary Education Data System](https://nces.ed.gov/ipeds/) - Mandatory annual reporting from every US college and university covering enrolment, completions, tuition, financial aid, staffing and institutional finance.
  <br><sub>National Center for Education Statistics · CSV, Access DB, REST API · ~5 GB · 6,000+ institutions reporting annually since 1980 · [US Government public domain](https://nces.ed.gov/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [College Scorecard](https://collegescorecard.ed.gov/data/) - Institution-level and field-of-study earnings, debt, completion and repayment outcomes linked from federal financial aid and tax records.
  <br><sub>US Department of Education · CSV, REST API · ~2 GB · 6,500+ institutions, 200,000+ field-of-study rows · [US Government public domain](https://collegescorecard.ed.gov/data/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [Common Core of Data](https://nces.ed.gov/ccd/) - The universe of US public schools and districts with enrolment by grade, race and gender, staffing counts, free lunch eligibility and geographic identifiers.
  <br><sub>National Center for Education Statistics · CSV, SAS, Fixed-width · ~10 GB · 100,000+ schools, 18,000+ districts annually · [US Government public domain](https://nces.ed.gov/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [Civil Rights Data Collection](https://civilrightsdata.ed.gov/) - School-level data on discipline, restraint, course access, chronic absenteeism and teacher experience, broken out by race, sex, disability and English learner status.
  <br><sub>US Department of Education Office for Civil Rights · CSV · ~5 GB · 17,000+ districts, 97,000+ schools · [US Government public domain](https://civilrightsdata.ed.gov/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [Stanford Education Data Archive](https://edopportunity.org/) - Test scores from every US school district placed on a single national scale, enabling like-for-like comparison of achievement and growth across states.
  <br><sub>Stanford University · CSV, Stata · ~2 GB · 13,000+ districts, 2009 onward · [CC BY-NC-SA 4.0](https://edopportunity.org/get-the-data/) · Free account required · Non-commercial only</sub>
