# Trade & Customs

> Import and export flows, tariff schedules, commodity classifications and port activity.

Part of [Awesome Datasets](../../README.md). Entries here are generated from the `dataset-*.json` files in this directory - edit those, then run `python3 scripts/build.py`.

## India

- [Ministry of Commerce Export Import Data Bank](https://tradestat.commerce.gov.in/) - Official Indian merchandise trade statistics queryable by commodity, country, port and time period, with HS-level detail.
  <br><sub>Ministry of Commerce and Industry · XLSX, CSV, Web query · Multi-GB · Monthly HS-level trade data since 1996 · [Government Open Data License - India](https://www.data.gov.in/government-open-data-license-india) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [Indian Customs ICEGATE](https://www.icegate.gov.in/) - Customs electronic gateway providing tariff enquiry, exchange rate notifications, trade guides and consignment tracking for Indian imports and exports.
  <br><sub>Central Board of Indirect Taxes and Customs · Web, PDF, REST API · Varies by service · National customs coverage · [Government terms of use](https://www.icegate.gov.in/) · Free account required · Commercial use: check terms</sub>
- [DGFT Trade Notifications and Data](https://www.dgft.gov.in/CP/) - Foreign trade policy, export incentive schemes, import and export licensing data and the ITC-HS commodity classification schedules.
  <br><sub>Directorate General of Foreign Trade · PDF, XLSX, Web · ~2 GB · Policy and licensing records · [Government Open Data License - India](https://www.data.gov.in/government-open-data-license-india) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [RBI Balance of Payments and Trade Statistics](https://data.rbi.org.in/) - Central bank statistics on merchandise and invisible trade, balance of payments, external debt and foreign investment flows.
  <br><sub>Reserve Bank of India · CSV, XLSX, Web query · Multi-GB · Quarterly and annual series since 1949 · [Free reuse with attribution to RBI](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/Copyright.aspx) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [World Integrated Trade Solution](https://wits.worldbank.org/) - Combined trade, tariff and non-tariff measure data for India and 200 other economies, drawing on Comtrade, TRAINS and WTO sources.
  <br><sub>World Bank, UNCTAD and WTO · CSV, REST API, XLSX · ~100 GB · Trade and tariff data from 1988 · [World Bank terms of use; CC BY 4.0 for most content](https://wits.worldbank.org/) · Free account required · Commercial use: check terms</sub>

## United States

- [Census USA Trade Online and API](https://www.census.gov/foreign-trade/data/index.html) - Official US merchandise import and export statistics by commodity, country, district and port at monthly frequency.
  <br><sub>US Census Bureau · CSV, REST API, XLSX · ~50 GB · Monthly detail by HS10 commodity since 1992 · [US Government public domain](https://www.census.gov/about/policies/open-gov.html) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [USITC DataWeb](https://dataweb.usitc.gov/) - Query interface over US trade and tariff data with import, export, tariff rate and trade balance detail, plus the Harmonized Tariff Schedule.
  <br><sub>US International Trade Commission · CSV, XLSX, REST API · Multi-GB · Trade data from 1989 onward · [US Government public domain](https://www.usitc.gov/) · Free account required · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [CBP Trade Statistics](https://www.cbp.gov/newsroom/stats) - Border and port operational statistics covering cargo volumes, entries processed, seizures and traveller crossings.
  <br><sub>US Customs and Border Protection · CSV, XLSX, Dashboard · ~1 GB · Monthly series by port of entry · [US Government public domain](https://www.cbp.gov/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [BTS Border Crossing Entry Data](https://www.bts.gov/browse-statistical-products-and-data/border-crossing-data/border-crossingentry-data) - Monthly counts of vehicles, containers, passengers and pedestrians entering the US at Canadian and Mexican border ports.
  <br><sub>Bureau of Transportation Statistics · CSV, REST API · ~200 MB · Monthly series by port since 1996 · [US Government public domain](https://www.bts.gov/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [UN Comtrade](https://comtradeplus.un.org/) - The global reference database of bilateral merchandise and services trade flows reported by nearly every country, harmonised to HS and SITC classifications.
  <br><sub>United Nations Statistics Division · CSV, REST API, JSON · ~1 TB · 5 billion+ trade records since 1962 · [UN Comtrade terms; free tier with limits, premium for bulk](https://comtradeplus.un.org/) · Free account required · Commercial use: check terms</sub>
