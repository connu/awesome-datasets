## European Union

- [Copernicus Data Space Ecosystem](https://dataspace.copernicus.eu/) - Free full-archive access to Sentinel-1 radar, Sentinel-2 optical, Sentinel-3 and Sentinel-5P imagery with STAC catalogues, S3 access and processing APIs.
  <br><sub>European Space Agency and European Commission · SAFE, GeoTIFF, NetCDF, STAC API · Tens of petabytes · Global coverage, Sentinel-2 revisit every 5 days · [Copernicus open licence, free and full reuse](https://dataspace.copernicus.eu/terms-and-conditions) · Free account required · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [Copernicus Climate Data Store](https://cds.climate.copernicus.eu/) - Climate reanalysis, seasonal forecast and observation datasets including ERA5, the most widely used global hourly atmospheric reanalysis.
  <br><sub>ECMWF and Copernicus Climate Change Service · NetCDF, GRIB, Python API · Petabyte-scale · ERA5 hourly global data from 1940 · [Copernicus licence, free reuse with attribution](https://cds.climate.copernicus.eu/) · Free account required · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [Copernicus Land Monitoring Service](https://land.copernicus.eu/en) - Pan-European land cover and land use products including CORINE Land Cover, imperviousness, forest type and high-resolution vegetation layers.
  <br><sub>European Environment Agency · GeoTIFF, Shapefile, GeoPackage · ~10 TB · Pan-European coverage from 1990 · [Copernicus open licence, free reuse with attribution](https://land.copernicus.eu/en) · Free account required · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [European Environment Agency Datahub](https://www.eea.europa.eu/en/datahub) - Environmental datasets on air quality, greenhouse gas emissions, water, biodiversity, noise and protected areas for all EEA member countries.
  <br><sub>European Environment Agency · CSV, GeoPackage, REST API, SPARQL · Multi-TB · Hundreds of environmental datasets · [EEA open data policy, CC BY 4.0 for most datasets](https://www.eea.europa.eu/en/legal-notice) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [ESA Earth Online](https://earth.esa.int/eogateway) - ESA's catalogue of heritage and third-party mission data including ERS, Envisat, Proba and commercial missions available under research licences.
  <br><sub>European Space Agency · GeoTIFF, HDF, NetCDF, SAFE · Petabyte-scale · Decades of Earth observation missions · [ESA data policy; varies from free to project-restricted](https://earth.esa.int/eogateway) · Free account required · Commercial use: check terms</sub>

## United States

- [USGS EarthExplorer](https://earthexplorer.usgs.gov/) - The primary access point for the full Landsat archive plus aerial photography, digital elevation and declassified satellite imagery going back to 1972.
  <br><sub>US Geological Survey · GeoTIFF, HDF, JPEG2000 · Multiple petabytes · 10 million+ Landsat scenes since 1972 · [US Government public domain](https://www.usgs.gov/information-policies-and-instructions/copyrights-and-credits) · Free account required · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [NASA Earthdata Search](https://search.earthdata.nasa.gov/) - Unified search and download across NASA's Earth science data centres covering atmosphere, land, ocean, cryosphere and human dimensions.
  <br><sub>NASA · HDF, NetCDF, GeoTIFF, Zarr, STAC API · Tens of petabytes · 10,000+ data collections · [NASA open data policy, free and open](https://www.earthdata.nasa.gov/engage/open-data-services-software-policies) · Free account required · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [Landsat Collection 2 on AWS](https://registry.opendata.aws/usgs-landsat/) - The full Landsat Collection 2 Level-1 and Level-2 archive staged in cloud object storage as cloud-optimised GeoTIFFs with a STAC catalogue.
  <br><sub>USGS and AWS Open Data · COG, STAC, JSON · ~2 PB · Full Landsat 1-9 archive · [US Government public domain](https://www.usgs.gov/information-policies-and-instructions/copyrights-and-credits) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [MRLC National Land Cover Database](https://www.mrlc.gov/) - Thirty-metre land cover, impervious surface and tree canopy rasters for the United States, updated on a consistent classification scheme since 2001.
  <br><sub>Multi-Resolution Land Characteristics Consortium · GeoTIFF, IMG · ~100 GB · Conterminous US coverage, multiple epochs · [US Government public domain](https://www.mrlc.gov/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [NOAA GOES Satellite Imagery](https://registry.opendata.aws/noaa-goes/) - Full-disk, continental and mesoscale imagery from the GOES-16 through GOES-19 geostationary satellites at up to 30-second refresh.
  <br><sub>NOAA and AWS Open Data · NetCDF, COG · Petabyte-scale · Continuous imagery since 2017 · [US Government public domain](https://www.noaa.gov/information-technology/open-data-dissemination) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
