# Government Spending & Budgets

> Public expenditure, procurement contracts, grants, subsidies and budget allocations.

Part of [Awesome Datasets](../../README.md). Entries here are generated from the `dataset-*.json` files in this directory - edit those, then run `python3 scripts/build.py`.

## United Kingdom

- [Contracts Finder](https://www.contractsfinder.service.gov.uk/Search) - Public sector procurement notices and awarded contracts above threshold, with supplier, value, dates and CPV classification, available as OCDS-formatted data.
  <br><sub>Cabinet Office · OCDS JSON, CSV, REST API · Multi-GB · Millions of notices and awards · [Open Government Licence v3.0](https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [HM Treasury Spending Data](https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/spending-over-25-000) - Departmental transparency releases listing every payment over £25,000, with supplier, date, amount and expense type.
  <br><sub>HM Treasury · CSV, XLSX · ~2 GB across departments · Monthly releases since 2010 · [Open Government Licence v3.0](https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [data.gov.uk](https://www.data.gov.uk/) - The UK's central open data catalogue covering spending, health, environment, transport, mapping and business data from central and local government.
  <br><sub>Cabinet Office · CSV, JSON, XML, WMS, REST API · Varies by dataset · 50,000+ datasets · [Mostly Open Government Licence v3.0](https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/) · Direct download · Commercial use: check terms</sub>
- [Office for Budget Responsibility Databank](https://obr.uk/data/) - Historical and forecast public finance series underlying official fiscal projections, covering receipts, spending, borrowing and debt.
  <br><sub>Office for Budget Responsibility · XLSX, CSV · ~500 MB · Long-run fiscal series with forecast vintages · [Open Government Licence v3.0](https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [Find a Tender Service](https://www.find-tender.service.gov.uk/Search) - High-value public procurement notices published under UK procurement regulations, the post-Brexit successor to the EU tenders journal.
  <br><sub>Cabinet Office · OCDS JSON, REST API, CSV · Multi-GB · Notices from 2021 onward · [Open Government Licence v3.0](https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>

## India

- [Union Budget of India](https://www.indiabudget.gov.in/) - The full annual union budget with demand-for-grants detail, expenditure profiles, receipts, subsidies and ministry-wise allocations in machine-readable form.
  <br><sub>Ministry of Finance · PDF, XLSX, CSV · ~2 GB per year · Annual budgets with scheme-level allocations · [Government Open Data License - India](https://www.data.gov.in/government-open-data-license-india) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [Central Public Procurement Portal](https://eprocure.gov.in/cppp/) - Tender notices, bid documents and award-of-contract details published by central ministries, PSUs and autonomous bodies.
  <br><sub>National Informatics Centre · PDF, XLSX, Web · Multi-GB · Millions of tenders since 2012 · [Government Open Data License - India](https://www.data.gov.in/government-open-data-license-india) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [Comptroller and Auditor General Audit Reports](https://cag.gov.in/en/audit-report) - Statutory audit reports on union and state government accounts, covering financial irregularities, scheme performance and public sector undertakings.
  <br><sub>Comptroller and Auditor General of India · PDF, XLSX · ~10 GB · Thousands of audit reports · [Government Open Data License - India](https://www.data.gov.in/government-open-data-license-india) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [RBI State Finances Study](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/AnnualPublications.aspx?head=State+Finances+%3A+A+Study+of+Budgets) - The annual comparative study of all Indian state budgets, with receipts, expenditure, deficits, debt and outstanding liabilities by state.
  <br><sub>Reserve Bank of India · PDF, XLSX · ~500 MB · Annual editions covering all states and union territories · [Free reuse with attribution to RBI](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/Copyright.aspx) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [data.gov.in Finance Sector](https://www.data.gov.in/sector/finance) - Open government catalogue of fiscal datasets covering tax collections, scheme expenditure, grants to states and public sector finances.
  <br><sub>National Informatics Centre · CSV, XLSX, JSON, REST API · Varies by resource · 4,000+ finance resources · [Government Open Data License - India](https://www.data.gov.in/government-open-data-license-india) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>

## United States

- [USAspending.gov](https://www.usaspending.gov/) - Every federal contract, grant, loan and direct payment with recipient, amount, awarding agency, place of performance and program code, exposed through a full REST API.
  <br><sub>US Department of the Treasury · CSV, REST API, JSON · ~1 TB full archive · 100 million+ award transactions since 2008 · [US Government public domain](https://www.usaspending.gov/about) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [SAM.gov Contract Opportunities](https://sam.gov/data-services) - Federal contract opportunities, entity registrations, exclusions and wage determinations, with bulk extracts and an API for procurement pipelines.
  <br><sub>US General Services Administration · CSV, JSON, XML, REST API · Multi-GB · Millions of opportunity and entity records · [US Government public domain](https://sam.gov/) · Free account required · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [Data.gov](https://data.gov/) - The United States' central open data catalogue, aggregating datasets from every federal agency plus many state and local governments.
  <br><sub>US General Services Administration · CSV, JSON, XML, GeoJSON, REST API · Varies by dataset · 300,000+ datasets · [Varies by publisher; federal data is public domain](https://data.gov/privacy-policy/#data_policy) · Direct download · Commercial use: check terms</sub>
- [Treasury Fiscal Service Federal Spending](https://fiscaldata.treasury.gov/datasets/) - Authoritative fiscal datasets including the monthly treasury statement, debt to the penny, federal revenue collections and outlays by function.
  <br><sub>US Department of the Treasury · REST API, CSV, JSON · API and bulk files · 80+ fiscal datasets · [US Government public domain](https://fiscaldata.treasury.gov/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [Federal Audit Clearinghouse](https://www.fac.gov/) - Single audit reports for every entity spending federal award money above the threshold, with findings, questioned costs and auditee financial detail.
  <br><sub>US General Services Administration · CSV, REST API, PDF · ~20 GB · 40,000+ audits per year since 1997 · [US Government public domain](https://www.fac.gov/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
