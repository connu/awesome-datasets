# Aviation & Flights

> Flight schedules, on-time performance, aircraft registries, air traffic movements and safety records.

Part of [Awesome Datasets](../../README.md). Entries here are generated from the `dataset-*.json` files in this directory - edit those, then run `python3 scripts/build.py`.

## Australia

- [ABS Tourism and Transport Statistics](https://www.abs.gov.au/statistics/industry/tourism-and-transport) - Official Australian statistics on air and sea passenger movements, overseas arrivals and departures, and transport industry activity.
  <br><sub>Australian Bureau of Statistics · XLSX, CSV, SDMX · ~1 GB · Monthly arrival and departure series · [CC BY 4.0](https://www.abs.gov.au/website-privacy-copyright-and-disclaimer) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [BOM Aviation Weather Services](https://www.bom.gov.au/aviation/) - Aerodrome forecasts, METARs, SIGMETs, wind and temperature charts and volcanic ash advisories for Australian airspace.
  <br><sub>Bureau of Meteorology · TAF, METAR, PDF, Web · Continuous feeds · All Australian aerodromes and FIRs · [CC BY 4.0](https://www.bom.gov.au/other/copyright.shtml) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [Airservices Australia Data](https://www.airservicesaustralia.com/) - Air traffic management data including movement counts by airport, noise monitoring, flight paths and aeronautical information publications.
  <br><sub>Airservices Australia · CSV, PDF, Web · Multi-GB · Movement and noise series across major airports · [CC BY 4.0 for most published data](https://www.airservicesaustralia.com/) · Direct download · Commercial use: check terms</sub>
- [data.gov.au Transport Datasets](https://data.gov.au/) - Australia's federal open data catalogue, including BITRE aviation activity, airport movements and transport infrastructure datasets with a CKAN API.
  <br><sub>Bureau of Infrastructure and Transport Research Economics · CSV, JSON, REST API · Varies by dataset · Hundreds of transport datasets · [CC BY 4.0](https://data.gov.au/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>

## European Union

- [EUROCONTROL Aviation Data for Research](https://www.eurocontrol.int/dashboard/rnd-data-archive) - Four-dimensional flight trajectories, flight plans and airport operational data for European airspace, released specifically for research use.
  <br><sub>EUROCONTROL · CSV, ZIP · ~100 GB · Millions of flights across sample months · [EUROCONTROL research data terms, free with registration](https://www.eurocontrol.int/dashboard/rnd-data-archive) · Free account required · Non-commercial only</sub>
- [EUROCONTROL Aviation Intelligence Portal](https://www.eurocontrol.int/our-data) - Pan-European air traffic statistics covering daily traffic counts, delays, punctuality, airport throughput and network performance.
  <br><sub>EUROCONTROL · CSV, XLSX, Dashboard · Multi-GB · Daily series across European airports and states · [EUROCONTROL terms, free reuse with attribution](https://www.eurocontrol.int/our-data) · Direct download · Commercial use: check terms</sub>
- [Eurostat Air Transport Statistics](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/web/transport/database) - Harmonised European air transport statistics on passengers, freight and aircraft movements by airport, route pair and country.
  <br><sub>Eurostat · TSV, SDMX, REST API · ~5 GB · Monthly and annual series for all EU airports · [Eurostat reuse policy, free with attribution](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/web/main/help/copyright-notice) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [EASA Safety Data and Publications](https://www.easa.europa.eu/en/domains/safety-management) - European aviation safety statistics, occurrence analysis, annual safety reviews and certification data for aircraft and operators.
  <br><sub>European Union Aviation Safety Agency · PDF, XLSX, CSV · ~1 GB · Annual safety datasets and occurrence summaries · [EASA reuse terms, free with attribution](https://www.easa.europa.eu/en) · Direct download · Commercial use: check terms</sub>
- [OpenSky Network European Coverage](https://opensky-network.org/datasets/) - Curated public datasets derived from the OpenSky sensor network, including COVID-19 flight impact, aircraft database and state vector samples with dense European coverage.
  <br><sub>OpenSky Network · CSV, Parquet, GZIP · ~500 GB · Curated multi-year flight datasets · [CC BY 4.0 for published datasets](https://opensky-network.org/about/terms-of-use) · Direct download · Commercial use: check terms</sub>

## United States

- [BTS On-Time Performance](https://www.transtats.bts.gov/ontime/) - Every scheduled domestic flight by a reporting US carrier with departure and arrival delay, cancellation, diversion and delay-cause breakdown.
  <br><sub>Bureau of Transportation Statistics · CSV, ZIP · ~30 GB · 200 million+ flights since 1987 · [US Government public domain](https://www.bts.gov/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [FAA Aircraft Registry](https://www.faa.gov/licenses_certificates/aircraft_certification/aircraft_registry/releasable_aircraft_download) - The releasable database of every US-registered civil aircraft with N-number, serial, make, model, engine, owner and airworthiness status.
  <br><sub>Federal Aviation Administration · CSV, ZIP · ~200 MB · 300,000+ registered aircraft · [US Government public domain](https://www.faa.gov/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [FAA Aviation Safety Information Analysis and Sharing](https://www.asias.faa.gov/) - Aviation safety data covering accidents, incidents, runway incursions, near midair collisions and service difficulty reports.
  <br><sub>Federal Aviation Administration · CSV, XLSX, Web query · Multi-GB · Decades of safety event records · [US Government public domain](https://www.faa.gov/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [NTSB Aviation Accident Database](https://www.ntsb.gov/Pages/AviationQueryV2.aspx) - Investigated civil aviation accidents and incidents since 1962 with aircraft, operation type, injuries, weather, phase of flight and probable cause.
  <br><sub>National Transportation Safety Board · CSV, JSON, XML, Access DB · ~1 GB · 90,000+ investigated events · [US Government public domain](https://www.ntsb.gov/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [OpenSky Network](https://opensky-network.org/) - Crowd-sourced ADS-B and Mode S air traffic surveillance data with raw and decoded state vectors, covering global flight trajectories at second-level resolution.
  <br><sub>OpenSky Network · REST API, Impala, CSV, Parquet · Petabyte-scale · Trillions of state vectors since 2013 · [Free for non-commercial research; CC BY-SA for published datasets](https://opensky-network.org/about/terms-of-use) · Free account required · Non-commercial only</sub>
