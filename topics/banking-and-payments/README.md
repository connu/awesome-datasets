## Brazil

- [Banco Central do Brasil Open Data Portal](https://dadosabertos.bcb.gov.br/) - The Brazilian central bank's open data portal with APIs for exchange rates, interest rates, credit aggregates, bank rankings and payment system volumes.
  <br><sub>Banco Central do Brasil · REST API, CSV, JSON, OData · Multi-GB · Hundreds of datasets with daily updates · [Free reuse with attribution, Brazilian open data policy](https://dadosabertos.bcb.gov.br/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [BCB Pix Statistics](https://www.bcb.gov.br/estabilidadefinanceira/estatisticaspix) - Transaction volumes, values, user counts and settlement statistics for Pix, Brazil's instant payment system and one of the most-adopted in the world.
  <br><sub>Banco Central do Brasil · CSV, XLSX, REST API · ~5 GB · Monthly and quarterly series since 2020 · [Free reuse with attribution, Brazilian open data policy](https://www.bcb.gov.br/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [BCB SGS Time Series Management System](https://www.bcb.gov.br/estatisticas) - The central bank's statistical series system covering monetary policy, credit, balance of payments, fiscal indicators and market expectations.
  <br><sub>Banco Central do Brasil · CSV, XLSX, REST API, OData · Multi-GB · 20,000+ economic time series · [Free reuse with attribution, Brazilian open data policy](https://www.bcb.gov.br/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [CVM Open Data](https://dados.cvm.gov.br/) - Brazilian securities regulator data covering listed company filings, investment fund daily returns and holdings, and registered intermediaries.
  <br><sub>Comissão de Valores Mobiliários · CSV, ZIP · ~100 GB · Daily fund data for 30,000+ funds · [Free reuse with attribution, Brazilian open data policy](https://dados.cvm.gov.br/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [Open Finance Brasil](https://openfinancebrasil.org.br/) - The technical standards, participant directory and public APIs for Brazil's open banking regime, including product and channel reference data.
  <br><sub>Open Finance Brasil governance structure · REST API, JSON, OpenAPI · API · 800+ participating institutions · [Open specifications; participant data under regime rules](https://openfinancebrasil.org.br/) · Direct download · Commercial use: check terms</sub>

## Germany

- [Deutsche Bundesbank Time Series Database](https://www.bundesbank.de/en/statistics) - The German central bank's statistical database covering money and banking, interest rates, balance of payments, securities holdings and payment systems.
  <br><sub>Deutsche Bundesbank · CSV, XLSX, SDMX, REST API · Multi-GB · 100,000+ time series · [Free reuse with attribution to Deutsche Bundesbank](https://www.bundesbank.de/en) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [ECB Data Portal](https://data.ecb.europa.eu/) - Euro area monetary, financial, banking supervision and payment statistics with an SDMX API, covering Germany alongside all euro area members.
  <br><sub>European Central Bank · CSV, SDMX, REST API, JSON · Multi-GB · Millions of series across 60+ datasets · [ECB terms, free reuse with attribution](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/services/disclaimer/html/index.en.html) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [BaFin Company Database](https://portal.mvp.bafin.de/database/InstInfo/) - The register of authorised German banks, insurers, investment firms and payment institutions with licence scope and identifiers.
  <br><sub>Bundesanstalt für Finanzdienstleistungsaufsicht · Web, CSV, XLSX · ~200 MB · Thousands of supervised institutions · [Free reuse with attribution to BaFin](https://www.bafin.de/EN/Homepage/homepage_node.html) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [EBA Risk Dashboard and Transparency Exercise](https://www.eba.europa.eu/risk-and-data-analysis/risk-analysis/risk-monitoring) - Bank-by-bank capital, asset quality, profitability and exposure data from EU-wide transparency exercises and stress tests, including German institutions.
  <br><sub>European Banking Authority · XLSX, CSV · ~5 GB · 100+ banks reported per exercise · [EBA terms, free reuse with attribution](https://www.eba.europa.eu/legal-notice) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [DESTATIS GENESIS-Online](https://genesis.destatis.de/datenbank/online/) - Germany's official statistics database covering finance, prices, business, population and trade, with a documented REST and SOAP API.
  <br><sub>Statistisches Bundesamt · CSV, XLSX, REST API · Varies by table · Hundreds of thousands of statistical tables · [DL-DE-BY-2.0](https://www.govdata.de/dl-de/by-2-0) · Free account required · Commercial use permitted</sub>

## United States

- [FFIEC Call Reports](https://cdr.ffiec.gov/public/) - Quarterly regulatory filings from every US bank with full balance sheet, income statement, loan composition, capital ratios and credit quality detail.
  <br><sub>Federal Financial Institutions Examination Council · CSV, XBRL, TXT · ~100 GB · 4,500+ banks filing quarterly since 1976 · [US Government public domain](https://cdr.ffiec.gov/public/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [FDIC BankFind Suite and API](https://banks.data.fdic.gov/docs/) - Institution directory, financial summaries, branch locations, failure history and merger records for every FDIC-insured bank.
  <br><sub>Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation · REST API, CSV, JSON · Multi-GB · Institution data from 1934 · [US Government public domain](https://www.fdic.gov/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [Federal Reserve Payments Study](https://www.federalreserve.gov/paymentsystems/fr-payments-study.htm) - Triennial and annual measurement of US noncash payment volumes and values across cards, ACH, checks and their fraud rates.
  <br><sub>Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System · XLSX, CSV, PDF · ~500 MB · Payment aggregates since 2000 · [US Government public domain](https://www.federalreserve.gov/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [CFPB Consumer Complaint Database](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/data-research/consumer-complaints/) - Consumer complaints about financial products with product, issue, company, state, narrative text and company response, updated daily.
  <br><sub>Consumer Financial Protection Bureau · CSV, JSON, REST API · ~3 GB · 6 million+ complaints since 2011 · [US Government public domain](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [NCUA Credit Union Call Report Data](https://ncua.gov/analysis/credit-union-corporate-call-report-data) - Quarterly financial and statistical filings from every federally insured credit union, with balance sheet, membership and delinquency detail.
  <br><sub>National Credit Union Administration · CSV, Access DB, ZIP · ~20 GB · 4,500+ credit unions filing quarterly · [US Government public domain](https://ncua.gov/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
