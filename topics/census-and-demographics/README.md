# Census & Demographics

> Population counts, household composition, migration and small-area socioeconomic characteristics.

Part of [Awesome Datasets](../../README.md). Entries here are generated from the `dataset-*.json` files in this directory - edit those, then run `python3 scripts/build.py`.

## India

- [Census of India](https://censusindia.gov.in/census.website/) - Decennial national census tables covering population, literacy, household amenities, migration, language and occupation down to village and ward level.
  <br><sub>Office of the Registrar General and Census Commissioner · XLSX, PDF, CSV · ~20 GB full tables · 1.2 billion+ people enumerated in 2011 · [Government Open Data License - India](https://www.data.gov.in/government-open-data-license-india) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [National Family Health Survey](https://dhsprogram.com/data/available-datasets.cfm) - Large-scale household survey of health, nutrition, fertility, anaemia and women's empowerment indicators, reported down to district level.
  <br><sub>International Institute for Population Sciences and The DHS Program · PDF, XLSX, Stata · ~5 GB · 600,000+ households in NFHS-5 · [Free for research; DHS data access terms apply](https://dhsprogram.com/data/available-datasets.cfm) · Free account required · Non-commercial only</sub>
- [MoSPI National Statistics](https://www.mospi.gov.in/) - India's central statistics office publishing national accounts, consumption expenditure, periodic labour force and price index data.
  <br><sub>Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation · XLSX, CSV, PDF · Varies by release · Hundreds of statistical releases · [Government Open Data License - India](https://www.data.gov.in/government-open-data-license-india) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [data.gov.in Open Government Data Platform](https://www.data.gov.in/) - India's central open data catalogue spanning every ministry, with a resource API and datasets covering demographics, agriculture, health and infrastructure.
  <br><sub>National Informatics Centre · CSV, XLSX, JSON, XML, REST API · Varies by resource · 600,000+ resources · [Government Open Data License - India](https://www.data.gov.in/government-open-data-license-india) · Free account required · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [IPUMS International India Samples](https://international.ipums.org/international/) - Harmonised census microdata samples for India alongside 100 other countries, with consistent variable definitions enabling cross-national comparison.
  <br><sub>IPUMS, University of Minnesota · CSV, Stata, SPSS, SAS · Varies by extract · Millions of person records per sample · [Free for research; redistribution prohibited](https://international.ipums.org/international/citation.shtml) · Free account required · Non-commercial only</sub>

## United States

- [US Census Bureau Data API](https://www.census.gov/data/developers/data-sets.html) - Programmatic access to the decennial census, American Community Survey, population estimates and economic surveys down to block group level.
  <br><sub>US Census Bureau · REST API, JSON, CSV · API · Hundreds of datasets, thousands of variables · [US Government public domain](https://www.census.gov/about/policies/open-gov.html) · Free account required · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [American Community Survey](https://www.census.gov/programs-surveys/acs) - Annual rolling survey of income, education, employment, housing, commuting, language and ancestry, the richest small-area socioeconomic source in the US.
  <br><sub>US Census Bureau · CSV, REST API, SAS · ~50 GB with microdata · 3.5 million households surveyed per year · [US Government public domain](https://www.census.gov/about/policies/open-gov.html) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [TIGER/Line Shapefiles](https://www.census.gov/geographies/mapping-files/time-series/geo/tiger-line-file.html) - The official geographic boundary files for states, counties, tracts, block groups, blocks, ZCTAs, roads and legislative districts, updated annually.
  <br><sub>US Census Bureau · Shapefile, GeoDatabase, GeoJSON · ~30 GB full set · National coverage at every census geography level · [US Government public domain](https://www.census.gov/about/policies/open-gov.html) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [IPUMS USA](https://usa.ipums.org/usa/) - Harmonised US census and ACS microdata from 1850 to the present, with consistent variable coding across 170 years that the raw files do not provide.
  <br><sub>IPUMS, University of Minnesota · CSV, Stata, SPSS, SAS, Parquet · Varies by extract · Billions of person records across samples · [Free for research and education; redistribution prohibited](https://usa.ipums.org/usa/terms.shtml) · Free account required · Non-commercial only</sub>
- [CDC WONDER](https://wonder.cdc.gov/) - Query system over national births, deaths, cancer incidence and population estimates, aggregated by geography, demographic group and cause.
  <br><sub>Centers for Disease Control and Prevention · TXT, CSV, REST API · Varies by query · Decades of vital statistics · [US Government public domain, with small-cell suppression rules](https://wonder.cdc.gov/datause.html) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
