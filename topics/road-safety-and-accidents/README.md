# Road Safety & Accidents

> Collision records, casualty severity, contributing factors and vehicle involvement in road traffic incidents.

Part of [Awesome Datasets](../../README.md). Entries here are generated from the `dataset-*.json` files in this directory - edit those, then run `python3 scripts/build.py`.

## France

- [ONISR Road Safety Data](https://www.onisr.securite-routiere.gouv.fr/) - France's national road safety observatory, publishing annual casualty counts, risk factors, vehicle categories and long-run trend analysis.
  <br><sub>Observatoire national interministériel de la sécurité routière · CSV, XLSX, PDF · ~500 MB · Annual series from 1970 · [Licence Ouverte 2.0](https://www.etalab.gouv.fr/licence-ouverte-open-licence/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [Bases de données accidents corporels (BAAC)](https://www.data.gouv.fr/datasets/bases-de-donnees-annuelles-des-accidents-corporels-de-la-circulation-routiere-annees-de-2005-a-2023/) - Every injury road collision recorded by French police since 2005, in four linked tables covering the crash, location, vehicles and each person involved.
  <br><sub>Ministère de l'Intérieur · CSV · ~1 GB · 1 million+ collisions since 2005 · [Licence Ouverte 2.0](https://www.etalab.gouv.fr/licence-ouverte-open-licence/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [Cerema Road and Mobility Data](https://www.cerema.fr/fr) - Technical research data on road infrastructure, traffic modelling, cycling networks and road safety engineering across the French network.
  <br><sub>Cerema · CSV, Shapefile, PDF · Varies by dataset · Hundreds of studies and datasets · [Licence Ouverte 2.0 for open publications](https://www.etalab.gouv.fr/licence-ouverte-open-licence/) · Direct download · Commercial use: check terms</sub>
- [Radars automatiques Open Data](https://www.data.gouv.fr/datasets/radars-automatiques/) - Location, type, direction and speed threshold of every automated speed camera in France, with installation dates.
  <br><sub>Ministère de l'Intérieur · CSV, JSON, GeoJSON · ~10 MB · 4,000+ speed cameras · [Licence Ouverte 2.0](https://www.etalab.gouv.fr/licence-ouverte-open-licence/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [Fichier des immatriculations et contrôle technique](https://www.data.gouv.fr/datasets/?q=controle+technique) - Open datasets on the French vehicle fleet and periodic roadworthiness testing, covering pass rates, defect categories and fleet age.
  <br><sub>Ministère de la Transition écologique · CSV, XLSX · ~2 GB · Millions of test records · [Licence Ouverte 2.0](https://www.etalab.gouv.fr/licence-ouverte-open-licence/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>

## United Kingdom

- [STATS19 Road Safety Data](https://www.data.gov.uk/dataset/cb7ae6f0-4be6-4935-9277-47e5ce24a11f/road-safety-data) - Every personal-injury road collision reported to police in Great Britain since 1979, with coordinates, casualty severity, vehicle manoeuvres and road conditions.
  <br><sub>Department for Transport · CSV · ~2 GB · Millions of collisions, vehicles and casualties · [Open Government Licence v3.0](https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [DfT Road Traffic Statistics](https://roadtraffic.dft.gov.uk/) - Annual average daily traffic counts by vehicle type at tens of thousands of count points on the British road network, downloadable per region or road.
  <br><sub>Department for Transport · CSV, REST API · ~1 GB · Count points from 1993 onwards · [Open Government Licence v3.0](https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [DVSA MOT Testing Data](https://www.data.gov.uk/dataset/e3939ef8-30c7-4ca8-9c7c-ad9475cc9b2f/anonymised-mot-tests-and-results) - Anonymised results of every MOT roadworthiness test, with make, model, mileage, test outcome and the specific defects recorded.
  <br><sub>Driver and Vehicle Standards Agency · CSV, TXT · ~20 GB · 500 million+ tests since 2005 · [Open Government Licence v3.0](https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [DfT Statistics Collections](https://www.gov.uk/government/organisations/department-for-transport/about/statistics) - Official transport statistics collections covering road casualties, vehicle licensing, walking and cycling, freight and public transport usage.
  <br><sub>Department for Transport · CSV, XLSX, ODS · Varies by collection · Hundreds of statistical series · [Open Government Licence v3.0](https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [data.gov.uk Transport](https://www.data.gov.uk/search?filters%5Btopic%5D=Transport) - National open data catalogue filtered to transport, covering traffic counts, cycling infrastructure, bus punctuality and local authority road data.
  <br><sub>Cabinet Office · CSV, JSON, GeoJSON, WMS · Varies by dataset · 3,000+ transport datasets · [Open Government Licence v3.0](https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>

## United States

- [FARS Fatality Analysis Reporting System](https://www.nhtsa.gov/research-data/fatality-analysis-reporting-system-fars) - A census of every fatal traffic crash on US public roads since 1975, with over a hundred coded variables per crash, vehicle and person involved.
  <br><sub>National Highway Traffic Safety Administration · CSV, SAS, DBF · ~5 GB full archive · ~35,000 fatal crashes per year since 1975 · [US Government public domain](https://www.nhtsa.gov/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [NHTSA Crash Report Sampling System](https://www.nhtsa.gov/crash-data-systems/crash-report-sampling-system) - A nationally representative probability sample of police-reported crashes of all severities, used to estimate total injuries and property damage.
  <br><sub>National Highway Traffic Safety Administration · CSV, SAS · ~2 GB · ~50,000 sampled crashes per year · [US Government public domain](https://www.nhtsa.gov/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [FMCSA Motor Carrier Safety Data](https://data.transportation.gov/) - Commercial vehicle safety records covering carrier registrations, roadside inspections, violations and large-truck crash involvement.
  <br><sub>Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration · CSV, JSON, REST API · Multi-GB · Millions of inspection and crash records · [US Government public domain](https://data.transportation.gov/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [NYC Motor Vehicle Collisions](https://data.cityofnewyork.us/Public-Safety/Motor-Vehicle-Collisions-Crashes/h9gi-nx95) - Every police-reported New York City collision since 2012 with coordinates, contributing factors, vehicle types and injury counts by road user type.
  <br><sub>NYC Police Department · CSV, JSON, GeoJSON, REST API · ~500 MB · 2 million+ collisions since 2012 · [NYC Open Data Terms of Use](https://opendata.cityofnewyork.us/overview/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [FHWA Highway Performance Monitoring System](https://www.fhwa.dot.gov/policyinformation/hpms.cfm) - National inventory of road extent, condition, geometry and traffic volume used for federal apportionment and exposure-adjusted safety analysis.
  <br><sub>Federal Highway Administration · Shapefile, CSV, Geodatabase · ~10 GB · National road network with annual updates · [US Government public domain](https://www.fhwa.dot.gov/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
