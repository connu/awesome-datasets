## India

- [CPCB Central Control Room for Air Quality](https://airquality.cpcb.gov.in/ccr/) - Live and historical readings from India's continuous ambient air quality monitoring network, reporting PM2.5, PM10, NO2, SO2, CO and ozone per station.
  <br><sub>Central Pollution Control Board · CSV, Web dashboard · Varies by query · 500+ monitoring stations across 250+ cities · [Government Open Data License - India](https://www.data.gov.in/government-open-data-license-india) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [OpenAQ India](https://openaq.org/) - Aggregated and normalised air quality measurements from government and research monitors worldwide, with India among the best-covered countries, exposed through an open API.
  <br><sub>OpenAQ · REST API, CSV, Parquet · Multi-TB archive on S3 · 2 billion+ measurements globally · [CC BY 4.0](https://docs.openaq.org/) · Free account required · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [data.gov.in Environment Sector](https://www.data.gov.in/sector/environment-and-forest) - Open government catalogue of environmental datasets covering ambient air quality by city and year, forest cover, water quality and pollution complaints.
  <br><sub>National Informatics Centre · CSV, XLSX, JSON, REST API · Varies by resource · 2,000+ environment resources · [Government Open Data License - India](https://www.data.gov.in/government-open-data-license-india) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [NASA Earthdata Air Quality](https://www.earthdata.nasa.gov/topics/atmosphere/air-quality) - Satellite-derived aerosol optical depth, NO2 and particulate estimates with global coverage, widely used to fill gaps where ground monitors are sparse.
  <br><sub>NASA · HDF, NetCDF, GeoTIFF, REST API · TB-scale · Daily global coverage since 2000 · [NASA open data policy, free and open](https://www.earthdata.nasa.gov/engage/open-data-services-software-policies) · Free account required · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [System of Air Quality and Weather Forecasting (SAFAR)](https://www.tropmet.res.in/) - Research-grade air quality and weather forecasting outputs for major Indian metros, produced by the Indian Institute of Tropical Meteorology.
  <br><sub>Indian Institute of Tropical Meteorology · CSV, PDF, Web dashboard · Varies by product · Multi-city forecast and observation series · [Research use; attribution requested](https://www.tropmet.res.in/) · Direct download · Commercial use: check terms</sub>

## United States

- [EPA Air Quality System (AQS)](https://www.epa.gov/aqs) - The federal repository of ambient air monitoring data, holding validated hourly, daily and annual pollutant measurements from thousands of state and local monitors.
  <br><sub>US Environmental Protection Agency · CSV, REST API · ~50 GB · Billions of measurements since 1957 · [US Government public domain](https://www.epa.gov/) · Free account required · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [AirNow API](https://docs.airnowapi.org/) - Real-time and forecast Air Quality Index observations by ZIP code, city or bounding box, the feed behind most US air quality apps.
  <br><sub>US EPA and partner agencies · REST API, JSON, CSV · API · 2,000+ reporting stations · [US Government public domain; API key required](https://docs.airnowapi.org/faq) · Free account required · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [EPA National Emissions Inventory](https://www.epa.gov/air-emissions-inventories/national-emissions-inventory-nei) - Triennial bottom-up inventory of criteria and hazardous air pollutant emissions from point, mobile, area and biogenic sources, resolved to county and facility level.
  <br><sub>US Environmental Protection Agency · CSV, Access DB · ~10 GB · Millions of emission source records · [US Government public domain](https://www.epa.gov/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [EPA Envirofacts](https://enviro.epa.gov/) - Unified query and API layer over EPA's regulatory databases covering air permits, toxic releases, water discharges, hazardous waste and facility compliance.
  <br><sub>US Environmental Protection Agency · REST API, CSV, JSON · Varies by system · Millions of regulated facility records · [US Government public domain](https://www.epa.gov/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [PurpleAir Real-Time Data](https://api.purpleair.com/) - Readings from a global network of low-cost consumer PM sensors, giving far denser spatial coverage than regulatory monitors at lower accuracy.
  <br><sub>PurpleAir · REST API, JSON, CSV · API · 30,000+ sensors worldwide · [PurpleAir API terms; free tier for non-commercial use](https://www2.purpleair.com/policies/terms-of-service) · Free account required · Commercial use: check terms</sub>
