## Spain

- [INE Tourism Statistics](https://www.ine.es/dyngs/INEbase/en/categoria.htm?c=Estadistica_P&cid=1254735576863) - Official Spanish tourism statistics covering visitor arrivals, tourist expenditure, hotel occupancy, rural and campsite accommodation by province.
  <br><sub>Instituto Nacional de Estadística · CSV, XLSX, JSON, REST API · Multi-GB · Monthly series by province and country of origin · [Free reuse with attribution to INE](https://www.ine.es/ss/Satellite?L=en_GB&c=Page&cid=1254735849170&p=1254735849170&pagename=Ayuda%2FINELayout) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [datos.gob.es](https://datos.gob.es/en) - Spain's national open data catalogue aggregating datasets from central, regional and municipal government, including tourism, transport and environment.
  <br><sub>Government of Spain · CSV, JSON, XML, RDF, REST API · Varies by dataset · 80,000+ datasets · [Varies; commonly CC BY 4.0](https://datos.gob.es/en) · Direct download · Commercial use: check terms</sub>
- [Inside Airbnb Spain Cities](https://insideairbnb.com/get-the-data/) - Scraped and cleaned snapshots of Airbnb listings for Barcelona, Madrid, Mallorca, Seville, Valencia and other cities, with price, availability, reviews and geography.
  <br><sub>Inside Airbnb · CSV, GeoJSON · ~10 GB across cities · Millions of listing snapshots worldwide · [CC0 1.0](https://insideairbnb.com/data-policies/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [Eurostat Tourism Database](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/web/tourism/database) - Harmonised European tourism statistics covering nights spent, occupancy, capacity and trips, comparable across all member states including Spain.
  <br><sub>Eurostat · TSV, SDMX, REST API · ~5 GB · Monthly and annual series for all EU countries · [Eurostat reuse policy, free with attribution](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/web/main/help/copyright-notice) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [AEMET OpenData](https://www.aemet.es/en/datos_abiertos/AEMET_OpenData) - Spanish meteorological agency open data with station observations, forecasts, beach and mountain conditions, widely combined with tourism demand modelling.
  <br><sub>Agencia Estatal de Meteorología · REST API, JSON, CSV · Varies by product · Hundreds of stations, continuous updates · [AEMET reuse licence, attribution required](https://www.aemet.es/en/nota_legal) · Free account required · Commercial use permitted</sub>

## United States

- [National Travel and Tourism Office Data](https://www.trade.gov/national-travel-and-tourism-office) - Official statistics on international visitor arrivals to the US, traveller characteristics, spending and outbound US travel by destination.
  <br><sub>International Trade Administration · CSV, XLSX, REST API · ~1 GB · Monthly arrival and spending series · [US Government public domain](https://www.trade.gov/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [BEA Travel and Tourism Satellite Accounts](https://www.bea.gov/data/special-topics/travel-and-tourism) - Economic accounts isolating tourism's contribution to GDP, employment and output by commodity and industry, consistent with the national accounts.
  <br><sub>Bureau of Economic Analysis · CSV, XLSX, REST API · ~500 MB · Quarterly and annual series from 1998 · [US Government public domain](https://www.bea.gov/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [National Park Service Visitor Use Statistics](https://irma.nps.gov/Stats/) - Monthly recreation visits, overnight stays and traffic counts for every unit of the National Park System, going back to 1904 for some parks.
  <br><sub>National Park Service · CSV, XLSX, REST API · ~500 MB · 400+ park units with monthly series · [US Government public domain](https://www.nps.gov/aboutus/disclaimer.htm) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [Inside Airbnb US Cities](https://insideairbnb.com/get-the-data/) - Listing-level snapshots for New York, Los Angeles, Chicago, Austin and other US markets with nightly price, minimum stay, host portfolio size and review history.
  <br><sub>Inside Airbnb · CSV, GeoJSON · ~10 GB across cities · Millions of listing snapshots · [CC0 1.0](https://insideairbnb.com/data-policies/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [BTS Airline Origin and Destination Survey](https://www.transtats.bts.gov/) - A 10 percent sample of airline tickets with itinerary, fare, carrier and coupon detail, the standard source for US air travel demand and pricing research.
  <br><sub>Bureau of Transportation Statistics · CSV, ZIP · ~50 GB · Quarterly samples since 1993 · [US Government public domain](https://www.bts.gov/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
