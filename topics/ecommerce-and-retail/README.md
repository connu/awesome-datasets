## Brazil

- [Olist Brazilian E-Commerce Public Dataset](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/olistbr/brazilian-ecommerce) - Real anonymised orders from a Brazilian marketplace with items, payments, freight, delivery timestamps, geolocation and customer review scores across nine relational tables.
  <br><sub>Olist · CSV · ~120 MB · 100,000 orders (2016-2018) · [CC BY-NC-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/) · Free account required · Non-commercial only</sub>
- [IBGE Pesquisa Mensal de Comércio](https://www.ibge.gov.br/estatisticas/economicas/comercio.html) - Official monthly retail trade volume and revenue indices by activity and state, Brazil's headline retail indicator.
  <br><sub>Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística · XLSX, CSV, REST API · Varies by series · Monthly series from 2000 · [Free use with attribution to IBGE](https://www.ibge.gov.br/acesso-informacao/institucional/copyright.html) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [IBGE SIDRA API](https://sidra.ibge.gov.br/) - Query interface and API over every IBGE aggregate table, covering retail, prices, production and services down to municipality level.
  <br><sub>Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística · REST API, CSV, XLSX · Varies by table · 8,000+ aggregate tables · [Free use with attribution to IBGE](https://www.ibge.gov.br/acesso-informacao/institucional/copyright.html) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [Portal Brasileiro de Dados Abertos](https://dados.gov.br/) - Federal open data catalogue aggregating datasets from every ministry, including commerce, consumer complaints, tax and procurement records.
  <br><sub>Government of Brazil · CSV, JSON, XML, REST API · Varies by dataset · 10,000+ datasets · [Varies by publisher; commonly CC BY](https://dados.gov.br/) · Direct download · Commercial use: check terms</sub>
- [Consumidor.gov.br Complaint Data](https://www.consumidor.gov.br/pages/dadosabertos/externo/) - Every consumer complaint filed against registered companies, with sector, problem type, company response, resolution status and satisfaction rating.
  <br><sub>Secretaria Nacional do Consumidor · CSV · ~2 GB · Millions of complaints since 2014 · [Open government data, attribution requested](https://www.consumidor.gov.br/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>

## United Kingdom

- [ONS Retail Sales Index](https://www.ons.gov.uk/businessindustryandtrade/retailindustry) - Monthly volume and value of retail sales by store type, with the internet sales share of total retail, the UK's headline consumer spending indicator.
  <br><sub>Office for National Statistics · CSV, XLSX, REST API · ~200 MB · Monthly series from 1988 · [Open Government Licence v3.0](https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [ONS E-commerce and ICT Activity](https://www.ons.gov.uk/businessindustryandtrade/itandinternetindustry) - Annual survey of business internet use, e-commerce sales value, website functionality, cloud adoption and broadband connectivity by industry and size band.
  <br><sub>Office for National Statistics · CSV, XLSX · ~100 MB · Annual series from 2008 · [Open Government Licence v3.0](https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [Food Hygiene Rating Scheme Open Data](https://ratings.food.gov.uk/open-data) - Hygiene inspection ratings, addresses, coordinates and business types for every food business in the UK, refreshed daily as open XML and JSON.
  <br><sub>Food Standards Agency · XML, JSON, REST API · ~500 MB · 600,000+ food businesses · [Open Government Licence v3.0](https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [ONS Consumer Price Inflation](https://www.ons.gov.uk/economy/inflationandpriceindices) - Item-level consumer price indices and the underlying price quotes collected in shops and online, published monthly with full item and region detail.
  <br><sub>Office for National Statistics · CSV, XLSX, REST API · ~2 GB · 180,000+ price quotes collected monthly · [Open Government Licence v3.0](https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [DEFRA Family Food Statistics](https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/family-food-statistics) - Annual household purchases of food and drink by quantity, spend and nutrient content, broken down by income group, region and household composition.
  <br><sub>Department for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs · ODS, CSV · ~200 MB · Annual series from 1974 · [Open Government Licence v3.0](https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>

## United States

- [Census Monthly Retail Trade Survey](https://www.census.gov/retail/index.html) - Official monthly retail and food services sales, inventories and e-commerce share by NAICS category, the headline US consumer spending indicator.
  <br><sub>US Census Bureau · CSV, XLSX, REST API · ~50 MB · Monthly series from 1992 · [US Government public domain](https://www.census.gov/about/policies/open-gov.html) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [BLS Consumer Price Index](https://www.bls.gov/cpi/data.htm) - Item-level price indices across hundreds of consumer goods and services categories, by region and metro area, with full historical revisions.
  <br><sub>Bureau of Labor Statistics · TXT, CSV, REST API · ~1 GB flat files · Monthly series from 1913 · [US Government public domain](https://www.bls.gov/bls/linksite.htm) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [USDA Branded Food Products Database](https://fdc.nal.usda.gov/download-datasets) - Label data for hundreds of thousands of branded grocery products including ingredients, serving sizes, nutrients, UPC codes and brand owner.
  <br><sub>USDA Agricultural Research Service · CSV, JSON, REST API · ~2 GB · 1.9 million+ food products · [US Government public domain](https://fdc.nal.usda.gov/api-guide.html) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [Census Annual Retail Trade Survey](https://www.census.gov/programs-surveys/arts.html) - Annual sales, e-commerce sales, inventories, purchases and gross margins for retail firms broken out by detailed industry.
  <br><sub>US Census Bureau · CSV, XLSX · ~100 MB · Annual series from 1951 · [US Government public domain](https://www.census.gov/about/policies/open-gov.html) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [Open Food Facts](https://world.openfoodfacts.org/data) - Crowd-sourced product database with barcodes, ingredients, allergens, nutrition facts, packaging and photos, downloadable in full.
  <br><sub>Open Food Facts · CSV, JSONL, MongoDB dump, Parquet · ~10 GB · 3 million+ products worldwide · [ODbL 1.0 (database), CC BY-SA 3.0 (contents)](https://world.openfoodfacts.org/terms-of-use) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
