# Elections & Voting

> Candidate results, constituency boundaries, turnout, campaign finance and electoral roll statistics.

Part of [Awesome Datasets](../../README.md). Entries here are generated from the `dataset-*.json` files in this directory - edit those, then run `python3 scripts/build.py`.

## India

- [Election Commission of India Statistical Reports](https://www.eci.gov.in/statistical-reports) - Official constituency-level results for every general and state assembly election, with candidate votes, party affiliation, electors and turnout.
  <br><sub>Election Commission of India · PDF, XLSX, CSV · ~10 GB full archive · All national and state elections since 1951 · [Government Open Data License - India](https://www.data.gov.in/government-open-data-license-india) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [Lok Dhaba](https://lokdhaba.ashoka.edu.in/) - Cleaned and machine-readable constituency-level results for Indian national and state elections, with candidate names, parties, votes and normalised party codes.
  <br><sub>Trivedi Centre for Political Data, Ashoka University · CSV, REST API · ~500 MB · All general and assembly elections since 1962 · [CC BY-NC-SA 4.0](https://lokdhaba.ashoka.edu.in/) · Direct download · Non-commercial only</sub>
- [Myneta Candidate Affidavits](https://myneta.info/) - Structured extracts from candidate self-declared affidavits covering assets, liabilities, criminal cases and education for national and state elections.
  <br><sub>Association for Democratic Reforms · CSV, Web · ~2 GB · 100,000+ candidate affidavits · [Free for non-commercial use with attribution](https://myneta.info/) · Direct download · Non-commercial only</sub>
- [ECI Electoral Statistics on data.gov.in](https://www.data.gov.in/catalogs) - Machine-readable electoral datasets published through the national open data catalogue, covering electors, polling stations and results summaries.
  <br><sub>National Informatics Centre · CSV, XLSX, JSON, REST API · Varies by resource · Thousands of catalogued resources · [Government Open Data License - India](https://www.data.gov.in/government-open-data-license-india) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [SHRUG Socioeconomic High-resolution Rural-Urban Geographic Platform](https://www.devdatalab.org/shrug) - Village and town level panel linking census, economic census, night lights, election results and administrative data through consistent geographic keys.
  <br><sub>Development Data Lab · CSV, Stata, Shapefile · ~10 GB · 600,000+ villages and towns · [Open for research use; see data agreement](https://www.devdatalab.org/shrug_download/) · Free account required · Commercial use: check terms</sub>

## United States

- [MIT Election Data and Science Lab](https://electionlab.mit.edu/data) - Cleaned and validated returns for presidential, senate, house and state elections at county and precinct level, harmonised across states and decades.
  <br><sub>MIT Election Data and Science Lab · CSV, Stata, RDS · ~5 GB · County and precinct returns from 1976 onward · [CC BY 4.0 with dataset-specific terms](https://electionlab.mit.edu/data) · Direct download · Commercial use: check terms</sub>
- [FEC Campaign Finance Data](https://www.fec.gov/data/) - Every federal campaign contribution, expenditure and committee filing, with itemised donor records, independent expenditures and candidate summaries.
  <br><sub>Federal Election Commission · CSV, REST API, Fixed-width · ~100 GB · Hundreds of millions of contribution records since 1980 · [US Government public domain](https://www.fec.gov/) · Free account required · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [Census Congressional District Boundaries](https://www.census.gov/geographies/mapping-files/time-series/geo/cartographic-boundary.html) - Cartographic boundary files for congressional districts, state legislative districts and voting districts, generalised for mapping and analysis.
  <br><sub>US Census Bureau · Shapefile, GeoJSON, KML · ~5 GB · All districts across congresses · [US Government public domain](https://www.census.gov/about/policies/open-gov.html) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [OpenSecrets Bulk Data](https://www.opensecrets.org/open-data/bulk-data) - Campaign finance records enriched with industry and sector classification, lobbying registrations, personal financial disclosures and revolving-door tracking.
  <br><sub>OpenSecrets · CSV, TXT, REST API · ~20 GB · Federal campaign and lobbying records since 1989 · [Free for academic and non-commercial use; commercial licence required](https://www.opensecrets.org/open-data/api-terms) · Free account required · Non-commercial only</sub>
- [Harvard Dataverse Elections Collections](https://dataverse.harvard.edu/) - Open repository hosting thousands of deposited political science datasets including election returns, survey waves, redistricting plans and polling archives.
  <br><sub>Harvard University · CSV, Stata, R, TAB · Varies by deposit · 100,000+ datasets across disciplines · [Varies by deposit; commonly CC0](https://dataverse.org/best-practices/dataverse-community-norms) · Direct download · Commercial use: check terms</sub>
