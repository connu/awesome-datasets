## United Kingdom

- [Find Case Law](https://caselaw.nationalarchives.gov.uk/) - The official archive of UK court and tribunal judgments as structured XML, with a documented API and bulk access for computational analysis.
  <br><sub>The National Archives · XML, REST API, Atom · ~50 GB · 100,000+ judgments from 2001 onward · [Open Justice Licence and OGL v3.0](https://caselaw.nationalarchives.gov.uk/about-this-service) · Direct download · Commercial use: check terms</sub>
- [BAILII](https://www.bailii.org/) - Free access to British and Irish primary legal materials including case law, legislation and law commission reports going back several centuries.
  <br><sub>British and Irish Legal Information Institute · HTML, PDF · ~100 GB · Millions of judgments and legislative documents · [Free for personal and non-commercial use; bulk reuse requires permission](https://www.bailii.org/bailii/copyright.html) · Direct download · Non-commercial only</sub>
- [legislation.gov.uk](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/) - The official database of UK legislation with full amended text, point-in-time versions and a comprehensive API returning Akoma Ntoso XML and RDF.
  <br><sub>The National Archives · XML, RDF, REST API, HTML · ~20 GB · All UK primary and secondary legislation from 1267 · [Open Government Licence v3.0](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/help) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [MoJ Court Statistics Quarterly](https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/civil-justice-statistics-quarterly) - Caseload, timeliness and outcome statistics for civil, family and administrative courts, with claim volumes, hearing delays and enforcement actions.
  <br><sub>Ministry of Justice · ODS, CSV, XLSX · ~1 GB · Quarterly series from 2007 · [Open Government Licence v3.0](https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [Employment Tribunal Decisions](https://www.gov.uk/employment-tribunal-decisions) - Full text of every published employment tribunal judgment, searchable by jurisdiction code, claimant, respondent and decision date.
  <br><sub>HM Courts and Tribunals Service · PDF, HTML · ~20 GB · 100,000+ published decisions since 2017 · [Open Government Licence v3.0](https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>

## India

- [National Judicial Data Grid](https://njdg.ecourts.gov.in/) - Live monitoring dashboard of pending and disposed cases across Indian district and high courts, broken down by case type, age and stage.
  <br><sub>eCourts, Department of Justice · Web dashboard, PDF, CSV · Varies by query · 50 million+ pending and disposed cases · [Government Open Data License - India](https://www.data.gov.in/government-open-data-license-india) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [Development Data Lab Indian Judicial Data](https://www.devdatalab.org/judicial-data) - Cleaned and anonymised metadata for tens of millions of district court cases scraped from eCourts, with judge, party, act, section and outcome fields.
  <br><sub>Development Data Lab · CSV, Parquet, Stata · ~50 GB · 77 million+ case records (2010-2018) · [Open for research use with attribution](https://www.devdatalab.org/judicial-data) · Direct download · Commercial use: check terms</sub>
- [India Code](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/) - The official digital repository of all central and state acts with amendments, subordinate legislation and section-level text.
  <br><sub>Ministry of Law and Justice · PDF, HTML, XML · ~20 GB · 800+ central acts and thousands of state acts · [Government Open Data License - India](https://www.data.gov.in/government-open-data-license-india) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [Supreme Court of India Judgments](https://www.sci.gov.in/judgements-judgement-date/) - Full-text judgments and orders of the Supreme Court of India, searchable by date, bench, petitioner and subject.
  <br><sub>Supreme Court of India · PDF, HTML · ~50 GB · Judgments from 1950 onward · [Government Open Data License - India](https://www.data.gov.in/government-open-data-license-india) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [eCourts Services](https://ecourts.gov.in/ecourts_home/) - Case status, cause lists, orders and judgments for district and taluka courts nationwide, the primary source behind Indian legal-tech datasets.
  <br><sub>eCommittee, Supreme Court of India · Web, PDF, REST API · Varies by query · 20,000+ courts covered · [Government Open Data License - India](https://www.data.gov.in/government-open-data-license-india) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>

## United States

- [CourtListener and RECAP Archive](https://www.courtlistener.com/help/api/) - Millions of federal and state court opinions, oral argument audio, judge profiles and PACER dockets, with bulk downloads and a full REST API.
  <br><sub>Free Law Project · REST API, JSON, CSV, XML · ~500 GB · 10 million+ opinions and dockets · [Public domain opinions; CC BY-SA for annotations](https://www.courtlistener.com/terms/) · Free account required · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [Federal Judicial Center Integrated Database](https://www.fjc.gov/research/idb) - Docket-level records of every civil and criminal case filed in US district courts since 1970, with nature of suit, jurisdiction, disposition and duration.
  <br><sub>Federal Judicial Center · CSV, SAS, TXT · ~5 GB · Millions of case records since 1970 · [US Government public domain](https://www.fjc.gov/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [US Courts Statistics and Reports](https://www.uscourts.gov/data-news) - Official judiciary caseload statistics covering filings, terminations, pending cases, bankruptcy and probation, by district and circuit.
  <br><sub>Administrative Office of the US Courts · XLSX, PDF, CSV · ~1 GB · Quarterly and annual tables · [US Government public domain](https://www.uscourts.gov/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [Caselaw Access Project](https://case.law/) - Digitised and structured US case law from 1658 to 2020, covering every officially published state and federal volume from the Harvard Law School Library.
  <br><sub>Harvard Law School Library Innovation Lab · JSON, CSV, XML, Static files · ~1 TB · 6.9 million cases, 40 million pages · [CC0 1.0 for case text](https://case.law/about/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [GovInfo Bulk Data](https://www.govinfo.gov/bulkdata) - Structured XML of federal statutes, the Code of Federal Regulations, the Federal Register, congressional bills and the US Code.
  <br><sub>US Government Publishing Office · XML, ZIP, REST API · ~200 GB · Full federal legislative and regulatory corpus · [US Government public domain](https://www.govinfo.gov/about/policies) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
