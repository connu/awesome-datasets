## United Kingdom

- [Ofcom Connected Nations](https://www.ofcom.org.uk/research-and-data/multi-sector-research/infrastructure-research) - Postcode-level fixed broadband and mobile coverage reporting by technology and speed band, published twice a year with underlying open data files.
  <br><sub>Ofcom · CSV, XLSX · ~5 GB · All UK postcodes, biannual since 2013 · [Open Government Licence v3.0](https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [Ofcom Data and Research](https://www.ofcom.org.uk/research-and-data) - Communications market research covering telecoms, broadcasting, post and online, including consumer usage, pricing and market share statistics.
  <br><sub>Ofcom · CSV, XLSX, PDF · Varies by publication · Hundreds of research outputs · [Open Government Licence v3.0](https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [Ofcom Spectrum Information System](https://www.ofcom.org.uk/spectrum/frequencies/spectrum-information-portal) - Register of UK spectrum licences and transmitter sites with frequency, location, power and licensee, including the wireless telegraphy register.
  <br><sub>Ofcom · CSV, XLSX, Web · ~2 GB · Hundreds of thousands of licence records · [Open Government Licence v3.0](https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [ONS Internet Access and Usage](https://www.ons.gov.uk/businessindustryandtrade/itandinternetindustry) - Official statistics on household internet access, e-commerce activity, digital skills and ICT use by businesses.
  <br><sub>Office for National Statistics · CSV, XLSX · Varies by series · Annual series · [Open Government Licence v3.0](https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [RIPE Atlas Measurements](https://atlas.ripe.net/) - Global network of hardware and software probes running continuous ping, traceroute, DNS and TLS measurements, with strong UK and European coverage and full API access.
  <br><sub>RIPE NCC · REST API, JSON · TB-scale archive · 12,000+ active probes worldwide · [CC BY-SA 4.0 for measurement results](https://atlas.ripe.net/legal/terms-conditions/) · Free account required · Commercial use permitted</sub>

## India

- [TRAI Performance Indicator Reports](https://www.trai.gov.in/) - Quarterly telecom subscriber counts, teledensity, broadband penetration, ARPU, minutes of use and internet subscriber breakdowns by circle and operator.
  <br><sub>Telecom Regulatory Authority of India · PDF, XLSX, CSV · ~2 GB · Quarterly series from 2005 · [Government Open Data License - India](https://www.data.gov.in/government-open-data-license-india) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [TRAI MySpeed Analytics](https://myspeed.trai.gov.in/) - Crowd-sourced mobile broadband speed measurements from the regulator's app, reporting download, upload and latency by operator, technology and state.
  <br><sub>Telecom Regulatory Authority of India · CSV, Web dashboard · ~5 GB · Millions of speed tests since 2016 · [Government Open Data License - India](https://www.data.gov.in/government-open-data-license-india) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [data.gov.in Communication Sector](https://www.data.gov.in/sector/communication) - Open government catalogue of telecom datasets covering subscriber bases, spectrum allocation, tower infrastructure, rural connectivity and postal services.
  <br><sub>National Informatics Centre · CSV, XLSX, JSON, REST API · Varies by resource · 1,500+ communication resources · [Government Open Data License - India](https://www.data.gov.in/government-open-data-license-india) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [Department of Telecommunications Annual Report](https://dot.gov.in/reportsstatistics) - National telecom statistics on network expansion, BharatNet rural fibre rollout, spectrum auctions, licensing and universal service obligation funding.
  <br><sub>Department of Telecommunications · PDF, XLSX · ~1 GB · Annual reports with state-level infrastructure tables · [Government Open Data License - India](https://www.data.gov.in/government-open-data-license-india) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [MeitY Digital India Statistics](https://www.meity.gov.in/) - Digital infrastructure and adoption statistics covering common service centres, digital payments volumes, internet penetration and electronics manufacturing.
  <br><sub>Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology · PDF, XLSX, Web dashboard · Varies by report · National and state-level digital adoption series · [Government Open Data License - India](https://www.data.gov.in/government-open-data-license-india) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>

## United States

- [FCC National Broadband Map](https://broadbandmap.fcc.gov/) - Location-level reported availability of fixed and mobile broadband by provider, technology and advertised speed, with challenge-process corrections.
  <br><sub>Federal Communications Commission · CSV, GeoPackage, REST API · ~100 GB · 114 million+ serviceable locations · [US Government public domain](https://www.fcc.gov/) · Free account required · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [FCC Measuring Broadband America](https://www.fcc.gov/general/measuring-broadband-america) - Panel measurements of actual delivered speed, latency, packet loss and web loading time from instrumented home routers across major ISPs.
  <br><sub>Federal Communications Commission · CSV, SQL · ~50 GB · Millions of measurements per year since 2011 · [US Government public domain](https://www.fcc.gov/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [M-Lab Open Internet Measurement Data](https://www.measurementlab.net/data/) - The largest open collection of internet performance tests, with per-test throughput, latency and traceroute data queryable in BigQuery.
  <br><sub>Measurement Lab · BigQuery, JSON, Parquet · Petabyte-scale · Billions of tests since 2009 · [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://www.measurementlab.net/data/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [FCC Universal Licensing System](https://www.fcc.gov/wireless/systems-utilities/universal-licensing-system) - The authoritative register of US wireless spectrum licences with licensee, frequency, geography, expiry and transaction history.
  <br><sub>Federal Communications Commission · Fixed-width, CSV, ZIP · ~20 GB · Millions of active and historical licences · [US Government public domain](https://www.fcc.gov/) · Direct download · Commercial use permitted</sub>
- [Ookla Open Data](https://registry.opendata.aws/speedtest-global-performance/) - Quarterly aggregated fixed and mobile speed test results tiled to web mercator zoom level 16, giving global download, upload and latency coverage.
  <br><sub>Ookla · Parquet, Shapefile · ~50 GB · Global tiles, quarterly since 2019 · [CC BY-NC-SA 4.0](https://github.com/teamookla/ookla-open-data) · Direct download · Non-commercial only</sub>
